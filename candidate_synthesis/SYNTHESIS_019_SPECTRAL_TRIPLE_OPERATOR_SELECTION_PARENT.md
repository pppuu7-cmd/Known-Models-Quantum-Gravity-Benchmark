## 3. A2 — reconstruction is not unique physical selection

The reconstruction theorem is conditional:

`given an admissible commutative spectral triple -> reconstruct the corresponding Riemannian spin geometry`.

It does not state

`physical principles -> one unique spectral triple`.

Before dynamics is fixed, a spectral parent still has to select or derive

- the algebra `A` and its representation;
- the Hilbert space / physical representation sector;
- the complete Dirac operator `D`, including finite/internal pieces;
- real structure, grading and other axiomatic data when relevant;
- the cutoff/profile function `f` or the finite set of moments entering the low-energy spectral action;
- the scale `Lambda`;
- the physical Lorentzian state/contour/causal prescription.

Different admissible choices generate different geometries, couplings and effective gravitational actions.

Thus

`A2 BLOCKED__SPECTRAL_TRIPLE_AXIOMS_RECONSTRUCT_GIVEN_GEOMETRY_BUT_DO_NOT_SELECT_UNIQUE_PHYSICAL_TRIPLE`.

## 4. Cutoff / moment freedom

In four dimensions the spectral-action expansion contains coefficients determined by moments such as `f_4`, `f_2`, `f_0` of the cutoff/profile function. These control, among other terms, cosmological, Einstein-Hilbert and gauge/curvature coefficients.

Unless the profile/moments are dynamically derived, a nontrivial part of the effective action remains constitutive input.

Choosing a regulator/profile after deciding the desired low-energy couplings is not a P4 origin law.
