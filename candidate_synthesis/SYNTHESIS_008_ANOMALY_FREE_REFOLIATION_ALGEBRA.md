## 3. A2 — closure is not a unique quantum representation

At the classical local metric level, strong assumptions on the undeformed hypersurface-deformation algebra can lead back to Einstein-type dynamics. This is a powerful rigidity control.

At the quantum level, however, the statement

`[C_I,C_J] closes without anomaly`

does not by itself select

- a unique Hilbert-space representation;
- a unique regulator/removal prescription;
- a unique operator ordering;
- a unique non-degenerate domain;
- a unique possible deformation of the algebra;
- a unique physical inner product / quantum measure.

Canonical LQG/AQG/master-constraint and related programs explore different ways of addressing precisely these choices.

Thus one has a fork:

1. **undeformed local classical rigidity** -> essentially returns to GR-type comparator dynamics;
2. **quantum/deformed realization** -> additional representation/deformation data must be physically selected.

Classification:

`A2 BLOCKED_AS_QUANTUM_ORIGIN_LAW__ANOMALY_FREE_CLOSURE_DOES_NOT_UNIQUELY_SELECT_THE_REPRESENTATION`.
