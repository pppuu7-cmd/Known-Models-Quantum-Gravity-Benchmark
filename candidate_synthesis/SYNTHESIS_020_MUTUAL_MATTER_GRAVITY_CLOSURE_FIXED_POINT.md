# SYNTHESIS-020 — Mutual Matter↔Gravity Closure Fixed Point

**Status:** REJECTED as P4 parent in causal/principal-symbol form / retained joint-selection lesson.  
**KMQGB iteration:** 140.  
**Purpose:** test whether the conditional input problem of constructive gravity can be removed by jointly selecting matter and gravity as a mutual consistency fixed point.

## 1. Proposed parent principle

Constructive gravity supplies a map, schematically,

`C_g : S_matter -> admissible S_gravity family`.

To avoid treating the matter action as arbitrary input, introduce a reverse physical consistency map

`C_m : S_gravity -> admissible S_matter family`

and demand a self-consistent pair

`S_g = C_g[S_m]`,

`S_m = C_m[S_g]`.

Tempting claim:

> the universe is the unique finite-freedom matter-gravity fixed point for which matter and geometry mutually determine each other's admissible dynamics.

This is stronger than ordinary gravitational closure because neither sector is intended to remain external input.

## 2. A1 — useful architecture, but the reverse matter selector is not supplied by causality alone

The gravity-side map has a concrete positive control in constructive-gravity closure.

The reverse direction is much less rigid. A given Lorentzian causal geometry admits many inequivalent quantum/classical matter actions satisfying

- the same causal cone;
- hyperbolicity;
- local covariance;
- ordinary unitary low-energy evolution;
- the same field representation.

Therefore a mutual-fixed-point slogan is not yet a complete microscopic rule until `C_m` is physically defined.

Classification:

`A1 PARTIAL_ONLY__GRAVITY_CLOSURE_MAP_EXISTS_BUT_REVERSE_MATTER_SELECTION_LAW_IS_NOT_DERIVED`.

## 3. Exact lower-order matter nullspace

A simple witness is a scalar field on a fixed Lorentzian metric:

`L_phi = -1/2 g^(mu nu) partial_mu phi partial_nu phi - V(phi)`.

For every admissible potential/function `V(phi)`, the principal symbol of the field equation is

`P(x,k) = g^(mu nu) k_mu k_nu`.

Thus the causal cone and leading hyperbolic propagation are independent of the entire lower-order function `V`.

A reverse selector based only on

- principal polynomial;
- characteristic cone;
- hyperbolicity;
- common foliation / initial-data compatibility

cannot distinguish the infinite family of potentials.

At increasing operator/order cutoff, analogous interaction freedom proliferates across matter fields.

Therefore

`causal matter compatibility != unique matter action`.

Classification:

`A2 FAIL__MUTUAL_CAUSAL_CLOSURE_LEAVES_INFINITE_LOWER_ORDER_MATTER_FUNCTIONAL_FREEDOM`.

## 4. Why feeding the freedom back into gravity does not remove it automatically

Even if `C_g[S_m]` responds to some lower-order matter data, the fixed-point problem still needs a **physical equation selecting those data**. Otherwise one has a family

`{S_m(lambda_i, functions...), S_g(lambda_i, functions...)}`

rather than a low-dimensional parent.

Demanding merely that both sectors be mutually compatible does not force the matter functions to one value unless a separate rigidity theorem is proven.

This is the joint-system version of the KMQGB transport-vs-origin problem.

## 5. Common attempted repairs and comparator pressure

A reverse matter selector based on

- RG/fixed-point criticality -> asymptotic-safety / QFT fixed-point comparator;
- spectral geometry -> spectral-action/noncommutative comparator;
- operator-measure minimization -> causal-fermion-systems comparator;
- anomaly cancellation / representation constraints -> strong selection rules but generally residual coupling/mass/Yukawa data remain;
- string consistency / modular invariance -> string comparator;
- random-matrix/entropy typicality -> matrix/black-hole comparator and envelope/cumulant freedom.

Thus adding a known matter-selection mechanism does not automatically create a new P4 parent.

## 6. A4 — no hard gravity datum before the joint fixed point is finite

Because the joint dynamical pair is not uniquely selected, SYNTHESIS-020 does not prospectively provide

- a unique interacting gravity operator;
- a comparator-orthogonal normalized `4g` hard datum;
- an all-point hierarchy;
- a same-parent CTP response;
- a detector-visible residual.

Classification:

`A4 BLOCKED__NO_FINITE_JOINT_PARENT_NO_UNIQUE_HARD_OBSERVABLE`.

## 7. Positive lesson

The constructive-gravity input problem is real and cannot be solved by simply replacing

`chosen matter -> derived gravity`

with

`mutually compatible matter and gravity`.

The next serious joint parent must derive **lower-order matter interactions as well as causal structure** from finite physical data.

A viable route needs a rigidity package strong enough to reduce both

- the gravitational operator freedom identified in Iter132;
- the matter interaction-function freedom identified here.

## 8. Reopen condition

Reopen mutual closure only when a concrete reverse law `C_m` is supplied and one can show prospectively that

1. its finite-cutoff functional freedom does not grow;
2. masses/couplings/potentials or their generating law are derived rather than fitted;
3. the coupled fixed-point pair is unique or low-dimensional;
4. the parent gives normalized hard spin-2 and matter predictions;
5. all-point + CTP completion follows;
6. comparator and Iter134 detector-visible tests are passed.
