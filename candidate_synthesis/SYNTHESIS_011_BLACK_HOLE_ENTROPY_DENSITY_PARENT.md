## 3. A2 — density of states does not determine the S-matrix

The coarse density `rho(E,J)` fixes only the **number** of states in an energy/angular-momentum band. A partial-wave S-matrix still depends on microscopic data such as

- exact resonance positions and widths;
- couplings/branching amplitudes to the two-graviton channel;
- relative phases;
- correlations among exponentially many microstates;
- elastic versus inelastic partitioning;
- possible non-resonant background phases.

Schematically, a resonance sum contains data of the form

`S_l(E) ~ background * product/sum over_n [E-E_n+i Gamma_n/2, g_n, phases, ...]`.

Knowing only the asymptotic count

`N(E,E+dE;l) ~ exp(S_BH(E,l)) dE`

does not fix `{E_n,Gamma_n,g_n,phase_n}`.

Hence many inequivalent unitary amplitudes share the same Bekenstein–Hawking coarse density.

Classification:

`A2 BLOCKED__BLACK_HOLE_ENTROPY_FIXES_COARSE_DENSITY_NOT_MICROSCOPIC_S_MATRIX_DATA`.
