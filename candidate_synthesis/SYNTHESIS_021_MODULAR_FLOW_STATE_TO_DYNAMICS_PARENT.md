**Status:** REJECTED as standalone P4 parent / retained exact state-to-flow positive control.

**Purpose:** test whether Tomita-Takesaki modular flow / thermal-time logic can provide the missing gravity-native state-to-transition law without choosing a Hamiltonian operator by hand.

## 1. Proposed parent capsule

Let `A` be a von Neumann algebra of physical observables in a generally covariant quantum theory and let `omega` be a faithful normal state. Tomita-Takesaki theory associates to the pair a modular automorphism group

`sigma_t^omega : A -> A`.

The Connes-Rovelli thermal-time hypothesis proposes interpreting this state-dependent modular flow as physical time flow in a generally covariant setting.

Tempting P4 principle:

> choose the fundamental gravity dynamics as the unique modular flow generated by the physical observable algebra and the physical quantum state.

This route is attractive after Iter130/132 because it attempts to derive dynamics from state/algebra data rather than choosing a Hamiltonian or differential operator directly.

## 2. A1 — exact downstream state-to-flow map exists

For a suitable algebra/state pair, modular theory gives a canonical one-parameter automorphism group. Thus

`(A, omega) -> modular flow`

is a genuine structural derivation rather than an arbitrary time-evolution ansatz.

This is an important positive control:

> a finite/conceptually compact specification of quantum-state structure can determine a nontrivial dynamical flow.

Classification:

`A1 PASS_CONTROL__ALGEBRA_PLUS_FAITHFUL_STATE_CANONICALLY_DETERMINE_MODULAR_FLOW`.

## 3. A2 — the state and algebra are primitive dynamical data

The modular flow is conditional on the pair `(A,omega)`.

Changing the faithful state generally changes the modular automorphism group. Changing the algebra/representation can change the flow and the available observables.

Therefore the construction answers

`given observable algebra and state, what modular flow follows?`

rather than

`what physical law uniquely selects the observable algebra and state?`

For a P4 parent, the freedom has moved upstream into

- the algebra of observables;
- representation/superselection sector;
- the physical faithful state;
- subsystem/region choice when a local algebra is used;
- the identification of modular parameter with operational proper/asymptotic time.

Classification:

`A2 BLOCKED__MODULAR_FLOW_IS_UNIQUE_CONDITIONAL_ON_UNSELECTED_ALGEBRA_STATE_DATA`.

## 4. Special local modular Hamiltonians do not close the general hard problem

In special QFT states/regions, modular flow has geometric or relatively simple generators. In generic interacting regions/states modular Hamiltonians can be highly nonlocal and state-dependent.

Therefore the existence of a modular Hamiltonian for a soft/causal-diamond sector does not automatically determine

- the UV/hard graviton transition operator;
- a normalized physical flat-space `4g` irreducible amplitude;
- the full all-point scattering hierarchy.

This is consistent with the Iter130 quantum-corner/state-law lesson: state structure is not yet a unique hard-transition law.

Classification:

`A3 FAIL_AS_NOVELTY_CERTIFICATE__MODULAR_THERMAL_TIME_ARCHITECTURE_ALREADY_REGISTERED`.

## 6. A4 — no new hard spin-2 datum from modular flow alone

Even after a modular flow is specified, KMQGB still requires

- a gravity-specific hard observable;
- absolute normalization;
- all-point/factorization structure;
- same-parent retarded/CTP response;
- comparator-orthogonal and detector-visible residual semantics.

The modular automorphism group alone does not supply these.

Classification:

`A4 BLOCKED__STATE_TO_FLOW_MAP_DOES_NOT_SELECT_COMPARATOR_ORTHOGONAL_HARD_GRAVITON_HIERARCHY`.
