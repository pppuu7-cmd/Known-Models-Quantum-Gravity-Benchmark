## 3. A2 — entropy does not fix the microscopic unitary matrix

Let the relevant black-hole microstate subspace have dimension

`d = exp(S_BH)`.

A unitary map on that subspace belongs to `U(d)` and contains order `d^2` real parameters before quotienting physically irrelevant conventions.

Knowing only

- `d` or the density of states;
- inclusive formation probability;
- inclusive decay probability;
- detailed-balance ratios;

fixes only coarse-grained combinations of matrix elements.

It does not determine the complete microscopic phases and rotations within the exponentially large microstate space.

Even fixing all transition **magnitudes** in a basis would in general leave phase/unitary-completion freedom subject to orthogonality constraints.

Therefore

`S_BH + unitarity + detailed balance`

does not by itself select a unique hard S-matrix.

Classification:

`A2 BLOCKED__BLACK_HOLE_ENTROPY_FIXES_DENSITY_RATES_NOT_MICROSCOPIC_PHASE_LAW`.
