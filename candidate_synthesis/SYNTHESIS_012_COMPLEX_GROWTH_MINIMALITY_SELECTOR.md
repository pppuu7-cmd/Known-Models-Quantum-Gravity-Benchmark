## 1. Proposed principle

Choose, among crossing/unitary gravitational amplitudes with the required GR infrared seed, the amplitude with the smallest allowed complex high-energy growth compatible with consistency.

This is attractive because a growth law could remove the contact-degree freedom left by hidden-zero and boundary-at-infinity consistency.

## 2. Local/rational branch — GR ceiling

Ordinary tree-level gravity exhibits the improved two-line BCFW behavior `M(z) ~ z^-2`. With vanishing boundary at infinity, finite factorization poles and the GR three-point seed reconstruct the ordinary Einstein tree amplitude.

Local higher-derivative contacts generically worsen complex/high-energy behavior and therefore lose a strict minimal-growth competition against the two-derivative GR control.

Thus, within the local rational same-spectrum branch,

`minimal constructible growth + GR three-point seed -> GR control`,

not a new KG parent.

## 3. Ultrasoft Regge branch — string ceiling

Recent 2026 amplitude bootstrap results establish a much stronger comparator at the opposite end. Under crossing/unitarity plus ultrasoft Regge behavior and minimal mandatory residue zeros, the minimally consistent solution collapses uniquely to Veneziano / Virasoro-Shapiro string amplitudes, with analogous higher-point extensions.

Therefore

`ultrasoft growth + minimal zeros -> string/dual-resonant comparator`.

## 4. A2 problem — what does “minimal” mean between the ceilings?

Between the GR polynomial/constructible regime and the string ultrasoft regime there is no unique ordering principle supplied by “minimal growth” alone.

One may choose

- a power-law exponent;
- an exponential order;
- an indicator function in different complex directions;
- a Regge trajectory class;
- a number/density of zeros or poles.

Unless these data are themselves derived from a finite physical law, the selector simply moves the missing hard datum into the chosen growth objective.

Classification:

`A2 BLOCKED__INTERMEDIATE_GROWTH_CLASS_NOT_PHYSICALLY_SELECTED`.

## 5. A3 comparator map

- minimal local/rational constructibility -> Einstein GR;
- ultrasoft + minimal zeros -> string/Virasoro-Shapiro;
- exponential/entire zero-free growth -> weakly-nonlocal/form-factor comparators;
- q-deformed/intermediate Regge families -> q-string/dual-resonant comparators where applicable.

Thus growth minimality alone has no demonstrated comparator-orthogonal window.

## 6. Positive lesson

A future P4 candidate **must declare its growth class prospectively**, but the growth class cannot be chosen merely because it yields a desired amplitude.

The missing origin law must derive at least one nontrivial quantity such as

- a complex order `rho`;
- an indicator/angular growth function;
- a Regge trajectory equation;
- a pole/zero density law;

from independent gravity-native data.

That derived quantity can then act as the degree/growth half of a joint zero-and-growth selector.
