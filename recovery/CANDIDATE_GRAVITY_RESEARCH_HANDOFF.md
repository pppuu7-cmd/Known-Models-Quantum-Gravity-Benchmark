## E. New design priors added by third-wave work

### E1 — Rigidity by overconstraint

Broad bespoke/Coon-like amplitude families show that individual spectral properties can remain tunable. More restrictive generalized-Virasoro assumptions can instead overdetermine the spectral recursion and select the string spectrum.

For KG: count remaining functions/parameters after consistency and comparator constraints. Prefer an isolated/low-dimensional solution manifold.

### E2 — Mediator attribution

Probe entanglement alone is not enough. Determine which physical channel carries the quantum information and explicitly profile C4/C6 ordinary quantized-matter/non-gravitational explanations.

### E3 — Quantum-channel readout

A gravity-induced channel should be characterized by transmissivity/response, added noise and whether it is entanglement-breaking. A non-EB result is stronger than one final entangled state, but still requires hidden-channel exclusion.

### E4 — Full crossover rather than endpoint

Use time/frequency/Planck-crossover shapes and linked normalization/contact terms rather than one UV exponent or one final statistic.

### E5 — Covariance is not constraint closure

Complete positivity and a covariant/path-integral formulation are important but do not replace a same-realization nonlinear Hamiltonian/momentum-constraint and physical-DOF audit.

### E6 — Geometry attribution

Indefinite causal order can be produced by ordinary quantum-control systems. If KG uses causal/process indefiniteness, it must demonstrate that the operational effect is tied to quantum geometry rather than an optical/controlled-order implementation.

### E7 — Regulator/continuum map

For discrete/numerical/truncated models, finite-regulator signals are not model-level observables until volume/coupling/truncation dependence and continuum/effective-theory interpretation are controlled.
