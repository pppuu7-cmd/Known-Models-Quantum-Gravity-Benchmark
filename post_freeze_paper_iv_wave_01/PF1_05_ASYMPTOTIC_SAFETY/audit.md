## 1. Anti-stale comparator update

The frozen benchmark must not use the obsolete claim that asymptotic safety lacks Lorentzian spectral information.

Assant, Litim & Reichert, *Spectral Functions of Lorentzian Quantum Gravity*, arXiv:2606.19321 (2026), compute graviton-mode spectral functions directly in Lorentzian functional RG, interpolating between classical GR and an asymptotically safe UV fixed point. The calculation incorporates symmetry/Ward conditions, finds normalisable spectral functions for several renormalisation-condition choices, and reconstructs the quantum effective action to quadratic order in curvature with graviton-induced form factors.

Earlier Lorentzian work also established a positive massless graviton peak plus multi-graviton continuum and asymptotically safe high-spectral behavior.

Therefore the following are now positive controls:

- direct Lorentzian graviton spectral function;
- explicit GR-IR to AS-UV interpolation at the propagator/spectral level;
- Ward-improved Lorentzian functional flow;
- nonlocal/form-factor information in the effective action;
- positive/normalisable spectral behavior in the studied prescriptions.

## 3. What remains open

The 2026 result itself reports consistent results for several sets of renormalisation conditions. Together with the general practical dependence of FRG calculations on truncation/closure, gauge and regulator handling, this means a terminal unique-asymptotic-safety residual needs additional authority.

For the strongest frozen regression require one compatible realization providing:

1. a declared physical renormalisation/trajectory prescription;
2. truncation/convergence or controlled error authority for the relevant observable;
3. gauge/BRST/Ward-complete physical mapping;
4. a same-realization contact/crossing-complete `4g`/higher or equivalent physical crossover observable when scattering attribution is claimed;
5. absolute normalization and Lorentzian state/analytic prescription;
6. same-domain GR/EFT and competing UV comparator predictions;
7. a robust residual invariant under the allowed methodological variations rather than one selected post hoc.

The currently audited Lorentzian `G2` plus curvature-form-factor layer substantially advances items 1–3 but does not by itself close the full chain above.
