## 1. Native framework object

A causal fermion system is specified by a Hilbert space, a set of finite-rank self-adjoint operators with bounded spin signature, and a positive measure on that operator space. Dynamics is selected by the causal action principle through its Euler–Lagrange equations.

This is a genuine microscopic/operator-valued spacetime architecture, so RQIR does not reject it for using non-metric native variables. The required operation is an **adapter** from CFS-native objects to declared RQIR observables.

## 2. Frozen-core adapter

For the controlled curved-spacetime continuum sector, use the following map:

`CFS causal-action EL equations`

`-> globally hyperbolic curved-spacetime continuum construction`

`-> coupled metric + Dirac dynamics`

`-> RQIR gravitational/matter response observables`

`-> Einstein–Dirac comparator in the identical continuum domain`.

No change to RQIR Core is required. The model's unusual microscopic representation is handled by the adapter layer exactly as intended by the v1.0 freeze.

## 3. Strong 2026 authority

Patrick Fischer and Felix Finster, *The Continuum Limit Analysis of Causal Fermion Systems for Curved Spacetimes*, arXiv:2605.30199 (2026), construct CFS for globally hyperbolic spacetimes starting from algebraic QFT and a quasi-free Hadamard state with a chart-independent ultraviolet regularization. Their central stated result is that, in the analyzed setup, the causal-action Euler–Lagrange equations are satisfied **if and only if the coupled Einstein–Dirac equations hold**.

This gives an unusually sharp frozen-core comparator statement:

`CFS controlled continuum sector = Einstein–Dirac observable dynamics`

within the theorem's assumptions/domain.

It is therefore not methodologically valid to treat the use of the causal action principle, operator-valued spacetime, or UV regularization by itself as a robust quantum-gravity residual in that domain.

## 4. Broader CFS dynamics is retained, not dismissed

The broader CFS programme goes beyond this continuum identity.

Relevant controls include:

- Finster, Kindermann & Treude, *Causal Fermion Systems: An Introduction to Fundamental Structures, Methods and Applications*, arXiv:2411.06450: the causal action principle is presented as the fundamental variational dynamics and the framework is intended to encompass non-smooth/quantum geometries and known low-energy limits.
- Dappiaggi, Finster, Kamran & Reintjes, *Holographic Mixing and Fock Space Dynamics of Causal Fermion Systems*, Annales Henri Poincaré (online 2025; volume 2026): in a limiting Minkowski construction, linearized CFS fields coupled to matter are represented by a unitary time evolution on fermionic/bosonic Fock spaces.
- Fischer & Paganini, arXiv:2504.19272: develops a relational interpretation of spacetime as correlations of a many-body quantum system beyond dependence on the continuum-limit ontology.

These results show that CFS is scientifically richer than the Einstein–Dirac continuum sub-sector. They do **not**, however, automatically supply the frozen RQIR object needed for a comparator-orthogonal Paper-IV residual.
