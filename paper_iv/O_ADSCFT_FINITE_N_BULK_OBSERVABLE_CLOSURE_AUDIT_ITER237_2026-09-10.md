## Question

For a fixed, explicitly specified AdS/CFT dual pair, can the exact/UV-complete boundary quantum theory already provide a terminal RQIR object

`boundary theory -> finite-N bulk quantum-gravity observable -> normalized common-domain GR/EFT comparator -> complete reconstruction/coupling/1/N error ledger`,

and can such a result represent the whole String/M-theory/holography family?

## A. Fixed-boundary microscopic definition — strong positive control

In explicit AdS/CFT examples, the boundary CFT supplies a well-defined quantum system whose partition functions, spectra and correlators are the fundamental observables. In the standard string-theory embedding, large-N/large-coupling regimes recover weakly curved gravitational/string backgrounds, while finite N and finite coupling encode quantum/string corrections.

Classification:

`PASS_SCOPED_FIXED_ADSCFT_BOUNDARY_QUANTUM_DEFINITION_AND_OBSERVABLE_ALGEBRA`.

This is qualitatively stronger than a framework that lacks any microscopic quantum object.

## B. Semiclassical bulk reconstruction — genuine but qualified

HKLL-type reconstruction and its interacting extensions recover perturbative bulk fields/correlators in suitable semiclassical code subspaces. Entanglement-wedge / operator-algebra quantum-error-correction language organizes the redundancy of this map.

The map is not an exact globally state-independent identification of sharply local bulk fields at finite N. Gravitational dressing, backreaction, finite entropy and nonperturbative `1/N` effects require a declared code/state sector and error notion.

Recent 2025–2026 literature contains explicit critiques/counterexamples to overly strong subregion-reconstruction statements at finite N and even discussions of discrepancies between alternative reconstructions. Those results do not invalidate AdS/CFT, but they prevent KMQGB from silently promoting a semiclassical reconstruction formula to an exact full-Hilbert-space bulk observable map.

Classification:

`PASS_SCOPED_SEMICLASSICAL_BULK_RECONSTRUCTION__BLOCKED_EXACT_FINITE_N_GLOBAL_LOCAL_BULK_OPERATOR_PROMOTION`.

## C. Exact boundary observable versus bulk gravity observable

RQIR does not require local bulk fields if an exact boundary observable can be compared in the same physical domain. A terminal branch object could therefore use, for example, a normalized boundary correlator/thermal observable whose bulk GR/EFT expansion and quantum/string corrections are calculable with controlled remainder.

What must not be done is mix:

- an exact finite-N boundary observable from one CFT/sector,
- a semiclassical bulk dictionary derived in another coupling/code regime,
- and a GR comparator with unmatched boundary conditions,

without an explicit interpolation and uncertainty map.

No single audited 2026 object was located that supplies all of the following simultaneously for a genuinely quantum-gravity-sensitive direction:

1. fixed CFT and global/asymptotic data;
2. exact or controlled finite-N observable;
3. explicit bulk gravitational interpretation over the comparison domain;
4. same-boundary-condition GR/EFT comparator;
5. controlled `1/N`, `1/lambda`, `alpha'`, nonperturbative and reconstruction errors;
6. a residual robust to allowed EFT/nuisance freedom.

## D. Scope ceiling

Even a terminal result for one AdS/CFT pair would close only that dual sector unless an explicit family-equivalence theorem maps other string/M-theory backgrounds and asymptotics onto the same RQIR object. AdS/CFT therefore cannot by itself disposition asymptotically flat string theory, de Sitter proposals, generic compactifications, BFSS/IKKT sectors or closed SFT.
