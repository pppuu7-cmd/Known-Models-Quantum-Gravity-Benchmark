### S2 — covariant closed/super string field theory

Sen–Zwiebach's 2024 review records consistent gauge-invariant string field theories for bosonic, heterotic and type-II strings, with BV structure, unitarity control and UV-finiteness under the stated contour prescription. Background-change equivalence is established locally/perturbatively in theory space for closed strings.

A 2026 analysis of background independence emphasizes that a manifestly background-independent formulation is not presently available for the quantum closed string in the strongest sense and that finite moves can encounter coordinate/description breakdowns.

Status:

`PARTIAL_SUBFAMILY_ONLY__QUANTUM_CLOSED_SFT_GAUGE_UNITARITY_STRUCTURE_PASS__GLOBAL_BACKGROUND_INDEPENDENT_OBSERVABLE_COVERAGE_OPEN`.

### S3 — AdS/CFT / gauge-gravity holography

For a specified boundary CFT and asymptotic sector, the boundary theory supplies a nonperturbative quantum object and many controlled bulk observables. Semiclassical bulk reconstruction, however, is code-subspace/background/order dependent; finite-N and gravitational-dressing corrections prevent treating local bulk fields as exact global observables.

Status:

`PARTIAL_SUBFAMILY_ONLY__FIXED_ADS_BOUNDARY_THEORY_NONPERTURBATIVE_DEFINITION_CONTROL__GLOBAL_BULK_OBSERVABLE_RECONSTRUCTION_AND_FLAT_DS_SCOPE_OPEN`.

### S4 — BFSS/BMN matrix quantum mechanics / M-theory sectors

BFSS provides a concrete matrix quantum mechanics proposed as DLCQ M-theory and has extensive low-energy supergravity/black-hole matching. BMN supplies controlled mass-deformed sectors and protected observables.

The definition is sector/limit specific: large-N and background/asymptotic recovery remain part of the claim and cannot be silently generalized to all M-theory backgrounds.

Status:

`PARTIAL_SUBFAMILY_ONLY__MATRIX_MTHEORY_SECTOR_AND_BLACK_HOLE_CONTROLS__GENERAL_BACKGROUND_AND_OBSERVABLE_COMPLETENESS_OPEN`.

### S5 — IKKT / IIB matrix model

Ho, Kawai and Steinacker, JHEP 2026, derive a low-energy local-field description from bilocal matrix fields, discuss emergent diffeomorphism invariance and unitarity, and identify the gravitational field in the IIB matrix model. This materially strengthens the branch beyond a generic spacetime-emergence conjecture.
