# Noncommutative spectral geometry — quantum-dynamics first-pass audit (Iter213)

**Date:** 2026-09-10  
**RQIR Core:** v1.0 FROZEN  
**Parent family:** `NONCOMMUTATIVE_SPECTRAL_GEOMETRY`

## Question

Does current noncommutative spectral geometry / spectral-action literature provide one fixed quantum-gravity realization containing

`quantum dynamics or measure for spectral geometry -> Lorentzian/physical gravity sector -> semiclassical GR map -> normalized gravity observable -> same-domain comparator -> propagated approximation/quantum/systematic error`

without silently combining incompatible constructions?

## Result in one sentence

No complete same-realization chain was located, but the old shorthand `no quantum dynamics exists` is too strong: several genuine quantum routes now exist and must be retained as scoped positive evidence.

## A. Classical/asymptotic spectral-action gravity control

The almost-commutative spectral action produces Einstein-Hilbert gravity together with higher-curvature/Weyl and Higgs-curvature terms after asymptotic expansion. Gravitational-wave and other phenomenology has been explicitly derived from this classical effective action.

This is a useful gravity/observable control but does not by itself quantize the geometry.

Classification:

`PASS_SCOPED_CLASSICAL_SPECTRAL_ACTION_GRAVITY__NOT_A_QUANTUM_GEOMETRY_CLOSURE`.

## B. Second quantization of spectral triples

The Chamseddine-Connes-van Suijlekom line constructs a fermionic second-quantized C*-dynamical system from a spectral triple and relates the entropy of its equilibrium state to a spectral action. Later expositions present this as one of the first routes toward a quantum theory of noncommutative geometry.

This is genuine quantum structure, but it does not yet constitute a path integral or dynamical measure over full gravitational spectral geometries with a detector-facing quantum-gravity observable and GR comparator.

Classification:

`PASS_SCOPED_SECOND_QUANTIZED_SPECTRAL_TRIPLE_STRUCTURE__BLOCKED_FULL_GRAVITY_GEOMETRY_DYNAMICS_OBSERVABLE_CHAIN`.

## C. Perturbative quantum spectral action

The spectral action has a perturbative one-loop quantization formulated in spectral/matrix language. One-loop counterterms can be kept in the spectral Chern-Simons/Yang-Mills form, giving generalized one-loop renormalizability.

However the modern account explicitly describes this as part of the first steps toward a quantum theory. The matrix-form construction used for explicit propagators/vertices assumes a flat four-dimensional Euclidean manifold to avoid the complications of the gravitational field, and the one-loop framework integrates matrix/gauge fluctuations around a fixed background. General power counting and diagrammatics beyond one loop remain open in the cited programme.

Classification:

`PASS_SCOPED_ONE_LOOP_QUANTUM_SPECTRAL_GAUGE_SECTOR__BLOCKED_FULL_DYNAMICAL_GRAVITY_SPECTRAL_GEOMETRY`.

## D. Random noncommutative geometry / Dirac ensembles

A distinct route does integrate over Dirac operators. Finite/fuzzy spectral triples turn the spectral action into convergent multi-matrix integrals; Dirac operators are dynamical variables and can be viewed as metric variables. FRG, Monte-Carlo, large-N, Schwinger-Dyson and bootstrap methods exist, and formulas are available for fuzzy geometries in several signatures/dimensions.

This is the strongest candidate for a genuinely dynamical spectral geometry.

But the audited literature remains in finite/fuzzy/toy regimes. Explicit solved/controlled phase-structure results are concentrated in low-dimensional or finite matrix geometries, and the 2025-2026 Dirac-ensemble literature still describes the programme as a toy-model route to quantum gravity. No audited same-realization result was located that establishes all of:

- a controlled 4D continuum limit;
- Lorentzian macroscopic geometry with a demonstrated Einstein/GR regime;
- physical scale/parameter ancestry from the Dirac ensemble to that regime;
- a normalized gravitational observable;
- an identical-domain GR/EFT comparator;
- propagated finite-N/large-N/truncation/continuum uncertainty.

Classification:

`PASS_SCOPED_RANDOM_DIRAC_GEOMETRY_QUANTUM_MEASURE__BLOCKED_4D_LORENTZIAN_GRAVITY_EMERGENCE_AND_OBSERVABLE_CLOSURE`.

## E. Same-realization composition guard

The four strands above cannot be concatenated by prose into one completed quantum-gravity model. In particular:

- classical almost-commutative gravity phenomenology does not supply the missing quantum measure;
- second-quantized entropy does not by itself supply a fluctuating gravitational geometry;
- one-loop matrix/gauge quantization around a fixed background does not supply a full gravity path integral;
- finite random Dirac ensembles do not inherit the classical 4D Lorentzian GR phenomenology unless an explicit continuum/reduction map is proven.

Cross-route splicing without such maps is forbidden under frozen RQIR same-realization discipline.
