## A. What is genuinely derived

I. Oda, arXiv:2605.01966v2, works in a manifestly covariant canonical formulation of quadratic gravity in de Donder gauge. Starting from

`S = integral sqrt(-g) [ R/(2 kappa^2) - alpha_C C^2 + alpha_R R^2 ]`,

the weak-field spectrum contains the massless graviton, a massive scalar and the standard negative-norm massive spin-2 mode.

The paper constructs BRST/anti-BRST transformations and shows that **if** the massive ghost belongs to an appropriate BRST quartet, the quartet contributes only zero-norm states to the physical Hilbert space, providing a route to physical-S-matrix unitarity.

Using the Bonora–Tonin superfield formalism, the paper derives an asymptotic massive-dipole equation for the ghost field and a Klein-Gordon-type equation for the proposed BRST-partner bound-state field.

The August follow-up, arXiv:2608.26085, strengthens the formal side: it formulates the effective asymptotic theory in a manifestly covariant/local canonical operator framework and obtains the same multipole-state Fock-space structure using both three-dimensional and four-dimensional Fourier quantizations.

## B. The decisive bound-state existence step remains an assumption

The May paper states explicitly that the key assumption for confinement is that the composite Heisenberg operator generated by the BRST transformation of the massive ghost has an asymptotic field corresponding to a bound state produced by the interaction between the massive spin-2 ghost and the Faddeev-Popov ghost.

It further states that without assuming such a bound state, the standard BRST analysis leaves the massive spin-2 ghost as a physical negative-norm state and unitarity is violated.

Therefore the logic is:

`bound-state pole exists -> BRST doublets/quartet -> zero-norm confinement -> physical-S-matrix unitarity`,

`quadratic-gravity dynamics -> bound-state pole exists`.

The first implication is the paper's derived mechanism. The second is the missing dynamical theorem.

## D. Relation to older BRST-confinement concerns

Earlier Oda work on Weyl-invariant gravity emphasized a structural difficulty for ordinary quadratic gravity: general-coordinate BRST symmetry acts on both the massive tensor and the graviton, so a naive quartet argument cannot simply distinguish and confine only the unwanted mode without further dynamical structure.

The 2026 quadratic-gravity construction attempts to supply such structure through the interacting composite bound state rather than a purely kinematic BRST assignment. This is a meaningful refinement, but precisely for that reason the existence and selectivity of the composite pole must be demonstrated rather than assumed.

A terminal certificate must show that the negative-norm massive spin-2 sector enters the quartet while the physical massless graviton remains in the nontrivial BRST cohomology.

`BLOCKED_SELECTIVE_GHOST_CONFINEMENT_VERSUS_MASSLESS_GRAVITON_COHOMOLOGY_CERTIFICATE`.

## E. August-2026 follow-up does not yet close the missing pole

The August paper derives a more detailed asymptotic effective Lagrangian and multipole-state quantization for the proposed confined sector. This strengthens internal consistency once the confinement configuration is admitted.

Thus it advances the **consequence side**, not the **existence side**, of the confinement chain.
