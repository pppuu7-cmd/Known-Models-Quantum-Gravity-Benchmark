## Question

Do current causal-set results close one same-realization chain

`fundamental quantum dynamics/measure -> suppression/selection of physically admissible causal sets -> controlled manifoldlike continuum -> Einstein/GR regime -> normalized genuinely causal-set gravitational observable -> same-domain GR/EFT comparator -> propagated discreteness/dynamics/continuum errors`?

## A. BDG action and Einstein-Hilbert continuum control — strong scoped PASS

The Benincasa-Dowker-Glaser (BDG) action is a concrete Lorentzian causal-set action whose continuum expectation on manifoldlike Poisson sprinklings reproduces the Einstein-Hilbert bulk structure in the appropriate limit, with boundary/corner structure being actively sharpened. This remains a strong same-domain comparator control for causal sets already known to approximate a manifold.

Classification:

`PASS_SCOPED_BDG_TO_EINSTEIN_HILBERT_CONTINUUM_IDENTITY`.

This does not yet determine which causal sets dominate the full quantum dynamics.

## B. Non-manifoldlike entropy suppression — major but incomplete progress

The causal-set path-sum literature has established strong suppression of broad entropically dominant non-manifoldlike classes, including layered/Kleitman-Rothschild-type orders, under BDG-like gravitational weights. Carlip's 2024 synthesis emphasizes that this is a substantial step toward continuum emergence.

However the remaining unsuppressed causal sets are not yet understood well enough to conclude that the dominant large-N sector is manifoldlike. Therefore suppression of known bad sectors is not equivalent to a proof of continuum emergence.

Classification:

`PASS_SCOPED_NONMANIFOLD_ENTROPY_SUPPRESSION__BLOCKED_REMAINING_UNSUPPRESSED_SECTOR`.

## C. Quantum sequential growth — 2026 update materially strengthens the dynamics census

Complex/quantum sequential growth (QSG) supplies a genuine quantum-measure/decoherence-functional route distinct from simply inserting the BDG action into a path sum. Earlier work established covariance/measure-extension criteria for large classes of complex growth models.

A 2026 result by Srivastava and Surya, accepted in Physical Review D on 11 August 2026, studies quantum Bell causality for non-commuting transition operators. The work derives strong algebraic constraints. For two natural operator orderings the transition algebra becomes commutative; a third ordering admits nontrivial relations but remains too complex for a general computable solution, and the simplest attempted two-dimensional Pauli realization is inconsistent. The authors describe this as a first step toward a non-commutative QSG realization.

This is important positive dynamics progress, but not yet a completed fundamental quantum dynamics producing a continuum gravity sector.

Classification:

`PASS_SCOPED_QSG_BELL_CAUSALITY_CONSTRAINTS__BLOCKED_COMPUTABLE_NONCOMMUTATIVE_DYNAMICS_AND_CONTINUUM_SELECTION`.

## D. 2026 quantum counting of the BDG action — computational advance, not a dynamics certificate

Adamson and Wallden's 2026 Physical Review Research paper gives an asymptotically efficient quantum algorithm for evaluating the BDG action of a supplied finite causal set. This is a real computational advance and may become useful for future large causal-set studies.

It does **not** define the physical quantum-gravity measure, select the ensemble, prove manifold emergence, or turn a classical description of a causal set into a quantum spacetime state. Therefore the word `quantum` in the algorithm must not be confused with a closure of quantum causal-set dynamics.

Classification:

`PASS_SCOPED_BDG_QUANTUM_COMPUTATION_RESOURCE__NO_PHYSICAL_DYNAMICS_PROMOTION`.

## E. Observable layer — substantial QFT-on-causet progress, but fixed-background scoped

Interacting quantum field theory on a fixed causal set now has explicit in-in/in-out correlators and scattering-amplitude constructions. These are genuine discrete observables and demonstrate that causal-set kinematics can support a nontrivial observable calculus.

But they treat the causal set background as supplied. They do not by themselves provide a gravitational observable averaged over or selected by the fundamental causal-set quantum dynamics. Hence they cannot close the family-level quantum-gravity residual.

Classification:

`PASS_SCOPED_QFT_ON_FIXED_CAUSAL_SET_OBSERVABLES__NO_DYNAMICAL_GRAVITY_OBSERVABLE_CLOSURE`.

## F. Embedding/manifold reconstruction — positive algorithmic progress

Recent embedding algorithms can reconstruct good Minkowski embeddings of suitable causal sets in dimensions including 2, 3 and 4. These are useful manifoldlikeness/reconstruction tools.

They do not solve the inverse dynamical question: whether the fundamental quantum measure overwhelmingly produces causal sets in the reconstructible manifoldlike class.

Classification:

`PASS_SCOPED_MANIFOLD_RECONSTRUCTION_TOOL__NO_DYNAMICAL_SELECTION_CERTIFICATE`.

## Same-realization discipline

The following shortcut is forbidden:

`BDG continuum identity on sprinkled manifolds + suppression of selected bad orders + QSG algebraic dynamics + QFT amplitudes on a fixed causet` -> `complete causal-set quantum gravity`.

These are currently different scoped ingredients. A complete RQIR object must show that one declared fundamental dynamics actually generates/selects a continuum sector in which the gravitational observable and comparator are defined.

## First-pass family result

Current causal-set theory is substantially stronger than a row described merely as `no dynamics` or `no observables`: it has concrete action, quantum-measure/growth candidates, entropy-suppression results, reconstruction tools, and fixed-causet observables.

But no audited same-realization chain was located that simultaneously closes:

1. a computable fundamental quantum dynamics/measure;
2. control of the remaining unsuppressed non-manifoldlike sector;
3. a large-N manifoldlike continuum selected by that dynamics;
4. an Einstein/GR regime in the selected ensemble;
5. a normalized genuinely gravitational causal-set observable;
6. a same-domain GR/EFT comparator;
7. discreteness, finite-N, measure/dynamics, reconstruction and observable error propagation.

Therefore the family remains:

`PARTIAL_SUBFAMILY_ONLY__ACTION_DYNAMICS_SUPPRESSION_AND_OBSERVABLE_CONTROLS__NO_SAME_REALIZATION_DYNAMICS_TO_MANIFOLD_GRAVITY_OBSERVABLE_CHAIN`.
