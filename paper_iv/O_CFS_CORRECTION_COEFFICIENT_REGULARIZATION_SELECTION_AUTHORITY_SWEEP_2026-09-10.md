## Question

Does the bounded primary/authoritative CFS corpus contain a causal-action-derived result that fixes at least one gravity correction coefficient/sign, or uniquely selects/removes the microscopic regularization freedom, sufficiently to build a normalized beyond-GR gravity observable with same-domain comparator and propagated uncertainty?

## Authority sweep

The sweep checked the current CFS research programme, the 2026 geometric Einstein derivation, the curved-spacetime continuum analysis, the continuum-limit/variable-regularization framework, the dynamical-gravitational-coupling route, the dark-matter/dark-energy route, the modified-measure route, and mathematical work on regularization/optimal-regularization constructions.

### A. 2026 direct geometric Einstein derivation

Finster–Krpoun (arXiv:2607.13871) strengthens the gravity bridge: a Lorentzian metric is induced, Einstein equations arise, gravitational coupling scales with the square of the regularization length, and a systematic expansion gives a procedure for higher corrections.

However the identified correction families are not evaluated into a unique parameter-closed gravity correction observable. The result is therefore a correction generator, not a coefficient/sign selection certificate.

### B. Curved-spacetime continuum control

Fischer–Finster (arXiv:2605.30199) provides a strong exact comparator control in the analyzed globally hyperbolic/Hadamard setting: the causal-action EL equations match coupled Einstein–Dirac equations. This closes the classical comparator side, not a finite-regularization residual.

### C. Variable regularization

The continuum-limit framework deliberately retains microscopic regularization dependence through a finite set of regularization parameters. The macroscopic form of the field equations can be robust while masses/couplings and correction coefficients remain sensitive to microscopic structure. This does not uniquely select the physical microscopic regularization.

### D. Optimal-regularization status

Mathematical work explicitly treats physically distinguished/optimal regularization as a target rather than a completed gravity-selection mechanism. Existing constructions and regularity studies do not provide a unique causal-action minimizer whose gravity correction coefficients, signs and error propagation are frozen for the required RQIR object.

### E. Dynamical gravitational coupling

The CFS research programme continues to list dynamical gravitational coupling as an active problem. The published modified-gravity route assumes microscopic Dirac/hyperbolic propagation; the programme itself states that this assumption requires direct causal-action justification or correction. Therefore its explicit phenomenology cannot yet be promoted to a fully causal-action-selected CFS residual.

### F. Cosmological and modified-measure routes

The dark-matter/dark-energy mechanism supplies nontrivial `T^-2` scaling, but its continuum-limit prefactors are empirical regularization parameters and their signs are not fixed. The modified-measure correspondence supplies an effective-theory route but not unique causal-action selection of one modified-gravity realization.
