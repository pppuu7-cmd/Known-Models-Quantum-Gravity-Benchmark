## Step 0 — frozen-judge integrity

A new additive wrapper was created at `protocol/PAPER_IV_D7_GLOBAL_DECISION_CONTRACT.json`.

It does **not** redefine D1-D7. It freezes the following decision hygiene:

1. BLOCKED, PARTIAL, undefined, or absent external objects are never silently converted to FAIL.
2. A child/subclass result is never promoted to family scope without an explicit family-scope proof or exhaustive material-subfamily resolution.
3. A merge is not a verbal similarity claim; it requires an explicit reduction/equivalence map with identity and double-counting control.
4. Operational proving-ground saturation and D7 protocol/infrastructure readiness are different from D7 scientific authorization.
5. Candidate Gravity R3 is independent and cannot be raised by repository engineering.
6. `NEW_REQUIRED` is forbidden while any major Tier-1 family remains unresolved or while an admissible existing/adapted/hybrid sufficient path has not been terminally ruled out.
