## Gate
Test the Iter251 next gate: whether one fixed weakly-nonlocal gravitational action is supported by an authority chain that simultaneously supplies (i) global chronology/CTC exclusion over the relevant solution space and (ii) a full momentum-space observable/comparator certificate with uncertainty propagation for that same realization.

## Evidence carried forward
Iter251 established that minimal matter gives only a scoped PASS for Einstein-sourced homogeneous Gödel solutions; nonminimal matter can retain Gödel CTC solutions; Shapiro/eikonal causality is not equivalent to global chronology; and no same-realization family-level certificate was identified.

## Iter252 targeted search result
A targeted search for a same-action closure chain did not identify a primary authority that closes both sides of the gate for one fixed action. The available strands remain separated:

1. causal/eikonal analyses can constrain Shapiro response or weak-coupling propagation without proving exclusion of all globally acausal exact solutions;
2. Gödel/exact-solution analyses are scoped to particular solution classes and couplings and do not supply a complete momentum-space comparator/error ledger;
3. propagator/unitarity/scattering analyses address pole structure, amplitudes or UV behavior, but do not by themselves establish global chronology protection for the same fixed realization.
