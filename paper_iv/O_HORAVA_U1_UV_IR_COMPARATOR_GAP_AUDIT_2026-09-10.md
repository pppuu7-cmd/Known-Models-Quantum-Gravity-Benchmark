## IR observable/comparator authority exists

Lin, Mukohyama, Wang & Zhu, *Post-Newtonian approximations in the Hořava-Lifshitz gravity with extra U(1) symmetry* (arXiv:1310.6666), construct a universal gravity-matter coupling for both projectable and non-projectable U(1)-extended Hořava gravity and derive the full set of PPN parameters in terms of theory couplings. They identify parameter regions satisfying Solar-System tests and regions reproducing GR PPN values.

This closes the claim that the U(1) branches have no normalized IR comparator-facing observable at all. A concrete IR PPN object exists for both projectability choices.

Scoped result:

`PASS_RQIR_GATE__HORAVA_U1_PROJECTABLE_AND_NONPROJECTABLE_NORMALIZED_IR_PPN_COMPARATOR_OBJECT_EXISTS`.

This does not establish UV completion or a same-realization UV→IR trajectory.

## Projectable U(1) branch

Hořava-Melby-Thompson (arXiv:1007.2410) and da Silva (arXiv:1009.4885) establish the enlarged U(1) gauge construction and scalar-mode elimination in the relevant classical formulations. However, the explicit complete 3+1 one-loop marginal beta-function system currently used by KMQGB belongs to ordinary projectable Hořava gravity (Barvinsky, Kurov & Sibiryakov, arXiv:2110.14688), not to the HMT/generalized-U(1) action with its extra fields, constraints and gauge symmetry.

No authenticated source found in this audit supplies a full 3+1 essential beta-function system and integrated AF/fixed-point trajectory for the U(1)-extended projectable action, propagated through the same realization into the PPN couplings of arXiv:1310.6666 with loop/truncation/matching remainder.

Projectable U(1) disposition:

`BLOCKED_MISSING_REQUIRED_OBJECT__HORAVA_PROJECTABLE_U1_3P1_QUANTUM_UV_TRAJECTORY_TO_PPN_COUPLINGS_SAME_REALIZATION_MAP`.

## Non-projectable U(1) branch

Mukohyama, Namba, Saitou & Watanabe (arXiv:1504.07357) show that the generic non-projectable U(1) action contains the scalar graviton; the scalar-free locus requires two couplings to vanish exactly even though their operators are marginal and expected to be generated by quantum corrections. This prevents the tuned scalar-free classical subcase from serving as a generic quantum authority without a protection theorem.

The recent explicit non-projectable path-integral/one-loop program for Hořava gravity is not an authenticated 3+1 U(1)-extended beta-function/trajectory calculation. Therefore no same-realization 3+1 quantum UV→IR chain to the U(1) PPN object is available from the sources audited here.

## Same-realization composition rule

It is forbidden to splice ordinary projectable Hořava beta functions into the U(1)-extended PPN realization simply because both theories contain similarly named couplings such as lambda. The U(1) theory changes gauge symmetry, field content and constraints. A matching theorem/decoupling limit with parameter normalization and error control would be required before composition.
