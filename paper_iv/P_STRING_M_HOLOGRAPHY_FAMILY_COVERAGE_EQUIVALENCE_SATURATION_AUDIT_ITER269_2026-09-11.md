## Authority synthesis
1. The existing PF1 string child already gives a concrete scoped result: the Virasoro–Shapiro/dual-resonance amplitude becomes rigid only under a sufficiently strong joint bootstrap package; weaker meromorphic/Regge/duality fingerprints are not unique. This is a real string-theory amplitude control, not family exhaustion.
2. Perturbative closed-string theory has a well-defined low-energy massless gravity sector described by supergravity/Einstein gravity plus higher-derivative `alpha'` and string-loop corrections in fixed backgrounds/compactifications. Therefore the row cannot be characterized as lacking any GR limit or calculable observable structure.
3. M-theory and the five superstring theories are related by established weak/strong and compactification dualities in specified regimes. These relations support a reduction rule where the same physical realization is explicitly connected, but they do not constitute a theorem that one audited background exhausts every material string/M realization.
4. AdS/CFT is a dual description of specified string/M-theory backgrounds. When a concrete holographic model is explicitly dual to a fixed string/M realization, it must not be counted as an independent extra Tier-1 theory for the same physical object; it is represented by the parent row through an explicit duality map. This reduction is domain-scoped: AdS/CFT does not by itself provide a general proof for arbitrary cosmological/flat/de Sitter backgrounds. Current 2026 holography reviews still treat extensions to dS/flat space as distinct developing directions.
5. Four-dimensional phenomenology requires a fixed compactification/vacuum, moduli stabilization and parameter transport. Quantum and perturbative corrections to the 4D effective action depend on compactification data and on `alpha'`/string-coupling expansions. A family-terminal RQIR object would therefore need the same realization from UV/string data to a normalized 4D gravity observable and propagated truncation/moduli uncertainty.
6. No current authority found in this audit supplies a single theorem or data capsule that exhausts all materially distinct string/M/holographic branches while also providing that same-realization 4D observable/comparator/error chain.
