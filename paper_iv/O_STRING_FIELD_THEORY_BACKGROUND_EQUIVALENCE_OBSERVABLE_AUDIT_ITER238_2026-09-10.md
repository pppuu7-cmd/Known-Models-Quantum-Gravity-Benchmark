## Question

Does quantum closed/super String Field Theory (SFT) provide a materially independent terminal quantum-gravity observable beyond the perturbative worldsheet/string-amplitude branch, and how much of the SFT branch can be reduced to that already-audited parent object?

## A. Gauge-invariant quantum theory — strong positive control

Sen and Zwiebach's 2024 review records consistent gauge-invariant string field theories for bosonic open/closed strings, heterotic strings and type-II strings. The framework uses BV quantization and can provide a proof of unitarity and a controlled account of UV finiteness under the stated integration-contour prescription.

Classification:

`PASS_SCOPED_SFT_GAUGE_BV_UNITARITY_UV_STRUCTURE`.

Thus SFT must not be described as a merely heuristic second-quantized string construction.

## B. Perturbative on-shell observable reduction rule

A central purpose of SFT is to reproduce the perturbative worldsheet string S-matrix while giving an off-shell/gauge-field-theoretic organization. Therefore, where an SFT result is only the same on-shell perturbative string amplitude in the same background and parameter regime, KMQGB does not count it as an independent family branch.

Reduction rule:

`SFT_ONSHELL_PERTURBATIVE_AMPLITUDE__SAME_BACKGROUND_AND_NORMALIZATION -> PERTURBATIVE_STRING_WORLD_SHEET_AMPLITUDE_SUBBENCHMARK`.

This avoids double counting the same physical observable merely because it has two formulations.

Classification:

`PASS_REDUCTION_GATE__PERTURBATIVE_SFT_SMATRIX_OUTPUTS_REDUCE_TO_EXISTING_STRING_AMPLITUDE_BRANCH_WHEN_REALIZATION_EQUIVALENCE_IS_EXPLICIT`.

## C. Background independence — real local theorem, incomplete global quantum closure

Classical and quantum closed-SFT background-independence results establish action/1PI equivalence under infinitesimal or suitably controlled background changes. The 2026 Kratky-Narra-Read analysis emphasizes both the genuine invariant structure and the limitations: finite background changes may run into singular/coordinate breakdown, and a manifestly background-independent formulation is not presently available for the quantum closed string in the strongest global sense.

This gives a precise result:

- the choice of infinitesimally nearby background is not simply an independent physical theory when the proven field-redefinition/BV map applies;
- this does **not** authorize a global quotient over arbitrary disconnected vacua, topologies, compactifications or asymptotics.

Classification:

`PASS_SCOPED_LOCAL_QUANTUM_BACKGROUND_EQUIVALENCE__BLOCKED_GLOBAL_BACKGROUND_SPACE_COMPLETENESS`.

## D. Independent SFT content that remains material

SFT remains independently relevant when it supplies content not already identical to a perturbative worldsheet S-matrix, including:

- nonperturbative classical solutions/tachyon condensation;
- off-shell amplitudes/effective actions;
- vacuum/background transitions not covered by the proven local equivalence patch;
- quantum observables sensitive to genuinely nonperturbative sectors.

For family-level RQIR closure, one such independent quantum-gravity sector must be tied to a normalized gravity observable, common-domain comparator and explicit truncation/background/contour errors.

No audited 2024–2026 authority supplies a single global quantum closed-SFT object covering arbitrary backgrounds with that detector/scattering/cosmological comparator package.
