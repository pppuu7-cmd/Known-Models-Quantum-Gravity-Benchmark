## Primary authority

Ritesh Srivastava and Sumati Surya, *Implementing Bell causality in quantum sequential growth*, accepted in **Physical Review D** on 11 August 2026, DOI `10.1103/thx5-dknv`.

## What is actually achieved

The paper studies quantum Bell causality (QBC) in quantum sequential growth (QSG) causal-set dynamics with non-commuting transition operators. Under a nonsingular dynamics:

- for the two most natural operator orderings of QBC, the transition-operator algebra is forced to become commutative;
- a third ordering, dependent on precursor-set size, yields additional nontrivial commutation relations and does not immediately force full commutativity;
- if a generator of the antichain subalgebra lies in its center, commutativity follows;
- the remaining noncommutative algebra is sufficiently complicated that the authors do not obtain a general form for the transition operators;
- the simplest attempted nontrivial `d=2` Pauli-matrix representation of the antichain generators is inconsistent.

The authors explicitly frame the work as a **first step** toward finding a noncommutative realization of QSG.
