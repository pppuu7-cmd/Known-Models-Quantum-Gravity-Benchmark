# O-Hořava family material-branch exhaustion audit

Date: 2026-09-10
Iteration: 194
RQIR Core: v1.0 FROZEN

## Question

Can the Hořava-Lifshitz Tier-1 row be represented by only the already tracked projectable and healthy non-projectable/BPS realizations, or are there additional materially distinct branches whose constraint/degree-of-freedom or UV/IR structure can change a Paper-IV terminal disposition?

## Frozen rule

A scoped child result cannot be promoted to family level unless all materially distinct branches are either (i) benchmarked to terminal disposition, (ii) reduced by an explicit physical equivalence/reduction map, or (iii) proven outside Paper-IV scope. BLOCKED is not FAIL and does not authorize NEW_REQUIRED.

## Primary-source branch census

### H0 — standard projectable Hořava gravity

Material and already tracked. Projectability changes the Hamiltonian constraint structure and leaves an extra scalar graviton. Current KMQGB authority already records a 3+1 asymptotically-free marginal-coupling trajectory, but the same-realization map through relevant IR couplings to a normalized extra-scalar observable remains missing.

Disposition: `TRACKED__BLOCKED_UV_TO_IR_RELEVANT_OPERATOR_AND_NORMALIZED_SCALAR_MAP`.

### H1 — healthy non-projectable/BPS Hořava gravity

Material and already tracked. The lapse depends on space, the action contains acceleration-vector operators, and the low-energy theory is a Lorentz-violating scalar-tensor/khronometric theory. Existing quantum results do not yet provide the required complete 3+1 same-realization UV RG trajectory through IR khronometric couplings with error control.

Disposition: `TRACKED__BLOCKED_3P1_UV_RG_TO_IR_KHRONOMETRIC_MAP`.

### H2 — projectable local-U(1) extension (Hořava-Melby-Thompson / generalized U(1))

This is materially distinct from H0, not a harmless reparameterization. The local U(1) enlarges the gauge symmetry and changes the constraint/physical-degree-of-freedom structure. Hořava & Melby-Thompson (Phys. Rev. D 82, 064027; arXiv:1007.2410) showed elimination of the scalar graviton in the original construction; da Silva (Class. Quant. Grav. 28, 055011; arXiv:1009.4885) showed the U(1) construction can be generalized without fixing lambda=1 while retaining scalar-mode elimination in that realization.

Because H0 generically contains the projectable scalar while H2 can remove it through an enlarged gauge symmetry, no identity map preserving physical state space/observables exists by default. H2 therefore cannot be reduced to H0 merely by setting couplings.

Disposition: `MATERIAL_INDEPENDENT_SUBBRANCH__BLOCKED_MISSING_U1_UV_TO_IR_NORMALIZED_OBSERVABLE_COMPARATOR_CERTIFICATE`.

### H3 — non-projectable local-U(1) extension

Also materially distinct. Mukohyama, Namba, Saitou & Watanabe (Phys. Rev. D 92, 024005; arXiv:1504.07357) performed a fully nonlinear Hamiltonian analysis and found that the scalar graviton is absent only when two particular couplings vanish exactly; the corresponding operators are marginal and therefore are expected to be generated by quantum corrections. Generic non-projectable U(1) Hořava therefore contains the scalar graviton.

This means the frequently quoted scalar-free tuned branch cannot serve as the generic quantum family representative without a symmetry/nonrenormalization theorem that protects those zero couplings. Conversely, the generic branch is not equivalent to H1 because the field/gauge/constraint content is enlarged by the U(1) sector.

Disposition: `MATERIAL_INDEPENDENT_SUBBRANCH__GENERIC_SCALAR_PRESENT__TUNED_SCALAR_FREE_NOT_QUANTUM_STABLE_WITHOUT_PROTECTION_THEOREM__BLOCKED_UV_TO_IR_COMPARATOR`.

### H4 — mixed-space/time-derivative Hořava extension

Material. Colombo, Gümrükçüoğlu & Sotiriou (Phys. Rev. D 92, 064037, 2015) introduced mixed derivative terms as a distinct power-counting class. Coates, Colombo, Gümrükçüoğlu & Sotiriou (Phys. Rev. D 94, 084014; arXiv:1604.04215) found that the minimal mixed-derivative gravity theory has four propagating degrees of freedom rather than the three of standard non-projectable Hořava gravity, with an additional scalar mode unstable at low energies. Klusoň (Phys. Rev. D 94, 104043; arXiv:1607.08361) independently confirmed an additional scalar degree of freedom by Hamiltonian analysis.

Thus H4 is not reducible to H1 over its full physical domain: it changes the kinetic/operator class and propagating spectrum. The low-energy instability is a scoped negative result for the minimal mixed-derivative realization, but cannot yet be promoted to every possible mixed-derivative completion.

Disposition: `MATERIAL_INDEPENDENT_SUBBRANCH__MINIMAL_REALIZATION_SCOPED_SCIENTIFIC_FAIL_LOW_ENERGY_INSTABILITY__FAMILY_COMPLETION_OPEN`.

## Non-core derived/hybrid variants

Arbitrary F(R), matter-enlarged, or other functionally extended Hořava models are not automatically additional required Tier-1 subbranches. They become material only if they define a concrete independent quantum parent with a distinct gravity/emergence claim and observable content under the existing Paper-IV promotion rule. The present audit therefore does not treat every phenomenological deformation as a mandatory branch.

## Branch-exhaustion result

The previous two-branch representation was insufficient. At minimum the Hořava family contains five material branch classes for Paper-IV governance:

1. H0 standard projectable;
2. H1 healthy non-projectable/BPS;
3. H2 projectable U(1)-extended;
4. H3 non-projectable U(1)-extended (generic and tuned scalar-free locus distinguished);
5. H4 mixed-derivative extension.

No family-level residual is defined by this census. H0/H1 remain BLOCKED by missing same-realization UV→IR matching objects; H2/H3 now require their own comparator-ready quantum/IR certificates; H4 has a scoped minimal-realization low-energy instability result but family completion remains open.
