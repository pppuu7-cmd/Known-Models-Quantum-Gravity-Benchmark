## Same-realization composition test

The UV marginal-flow authority and the low-energy FRG/phenomenology authorities cannot be composed automatically. They do not presently supply a single published object containing all of:

- one fixed microscopic projectable action and normalization convention;
- marginal AF trajectory data;
- running/matching of the relevant lower-derivative operators through the Lifshitz-to-relativistic crossover;
- an explicit map between the UV coupling basis and the IR scalar-sector normalization;
- propagated loop/truncation/matching uncertainty or controlled remainder;
- a normalized detector/observable-facing scalar quantity in the same realization;
- a common-domain GR/EFT comparator.

A phenomenological choice of IR lambda, an independent low-energy FRG trajectory, or an IR scalar dispersion relation is not a substitute for this ancestry certificate.
