## A. The branch is substantially more developed than a mere ghost reinterpretation

K. Sravan Kumar and J. Marto, arXiv:2603.07150v3 (18 June 2026), fix the sign choice of the Weyl-squared sector so that the additional spin-2 mode is treated as a dual-IHO-like instability with spacelike momentum support. The paper embeds the construction in direct-sum quantum field theory (DQFT), excludes the spin-2 mode from the asymptotic spectrum, discusses scattering amplitudes, one-loop renormalization/RG and derives inflationary observables.

The shorter follow-up, arXiv:2604.19707v3 (7 July 2026), formulates the core unitarity argument more sharply: using the Wightman spectrum condition and Kallen-Lehmann support, the authors argue that the dual-IHO spectral density vanishes and that the principal-value propagator follows as a theorem rather than an externally imposed fakeon prescription. The authors further claim that Cutkosky cuts of the dual-IHO mode vanish, that the optical theorem is preserved, and that the virtual mode contributes at all loop orders without becoming an asymptotic state.

KMQGB therefore upgrades the branch from a generic speculative pole reinterpretation to:

`PROVISIONAL_STRONG_SAME_PROGRAMME_THEORY_CHAIN`.

This positive control covers more of the frozen RQIR chain than most unresolved pole proposals.

## B. Same-programme physical observable exists

The long paper derives an explicit modification of the Starobinsky-like tensor-to-scalar ratio,

`r ~= (12/N^2) * [12 alpha / (12 alpha - beta)]`,

under its stated convention/sign regime, and supplies representative numerical values. It also proposes parity-asymmetric large-angle primordial spectra associated with the DQFT/direct-sum inflation construction.

The key RQIR point is not whether these observables are already experimentally supported. It is that the branch does possess a normalized quantitative observable layer downstream of the same proposed spin-2 construction.

Classification:

`PASS_PROVISIONAL_SAME_PROGRAMME_QUANTITATIVE_INFLATIONARY_OBSERVABLE_CONTROL`.

This is stronger than an action-only or pole-only claim.

## C. Lorentz covariance is addressed internally, but not independently closed

A spacelike pole raises an immediate frame-dependence concern if one defines the sector by a frame-specific split in spatial momentum. The long paper explicitly recognizes this issue and argues that the physical classification is instead by the Lorentz-invariant sign of `k^2`; the full spacelike Lorentz orbit is taken as the object, not a frame-dependent momentum interval.

This is an important internal response and means KMQGB should not label the proposal trivially Lorentz noncovariant merely because the pole is spacelike.

However, internal covariance arguments do not yet provide an independent interacting-theory certificate. In particular, a terminal authority would need to establish that the DQFT superselection construction, the PV propagator and the matter/gravity vertices preserve the required Ward/BRST identities, locality/causal support conditions and equivalence-principle structure in the interacting theory.

Classification:

`PASS_PROVISIONAL_INTERNAL_LORENTZ_ORBIT_ARGUMENT__BLOCKED_INDEPENDENT_INTERACTING_COVARIANCE_VALIDATION`.

## D. Matter coupling / effective decoupling is claimed but is a decisive validation target

The long paper includes ordinary matter in the quadratic-gravity setting and argues that the dual-IHO spin-2 remains off shell and effectively decoupled from ordinary matter as an asymptotic degree of freedom. This is exactly the type of property required to avoid negative-probability production channels.

But `not an asymptotic particle` is not by itself sufficient to establish that all interacting matter amplitudes are free of Lorentz, causality or equivalence-principle pathologies. The relevant real/dispersive virtual contributions must satisfy the full interacting identities under the same propagator theorem.

KMQGB therefore freezes the matter statement as an author-derived claim, not as an externally established theorem:

`PROVISIONAL_INTERACTING_MATTER_DECOUPLING_CLAIM`.

## E. Jodlowski 2026 is a mandatory nearby negative control, not a direct refutation

K. Jodlowski, Phys. Rev. D 113, 065016 (2026), proves two fatal obstructions for covariant interacting virtual tachyons in the fakeon/Wheeler-type setting: Lorentz boosts mix creation/annihilation operators in the canonical construction, and the relevant Feynman-real-part / Wheeler supports do not permit the intended purely virtual prescription. Couplings to stable Standard Model matter further lead to Lorentz/equivalence-principle problems.

This result cannot be silently promoted to a no-go theorem for dual-IHO because Kumar-Marto intentionally use a materially different object: a spacelike pole with a PV propagator claimed to follow from spectral support/non-normalizable-vacuum structure, not a timelike fakeon/Feynman-Wheeler virtual particle.

Conversely, merely asserting that distinction does not settle the issue. A robust dual-IHO paper must show explicitly why the Jodlowski obstructions do not reappear after interactions, boosts and matter coupling in the DQFT realization.

Classification:

`MANDATORY_NEARBY_NEGATIVE_CONTROL__NOT_YET_DIRECTLY_DISPOSITIONED_BY_INDEPENDENT_AUTHORITY`.
