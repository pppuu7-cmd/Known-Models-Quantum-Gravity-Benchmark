## A. Second quantization of spectral triples

The Chamseddine-Connes-van Suijlekom / Dong-Khalkhali-van Suijlekom programme is genuine quantum structure. It passes from a spectral triple to a second-quantized C*-dynamical system, constructs the Fock/Clifford-algebra level, and relates equilibrium entropy/energy to spectral-action functionals.

This does **not** by itself supply a quantum measure over gravitational Dirac operators or a fluctuating 4D Lorentzian metric geometry. The starting spectral triple and its Dirac generator remain the input defining the second-quantized system. Therefore the result quantizes fermionic/multiparticle content associated with spectral geometry, not the full gravitational geometry required by the RQIR family object.

Classification:

`PASS_SCOPED_SECOND_QUANTIZATION__NO_FULL_GRAVITATIONAL_SPECTRAL_GEOMETRY_MEASURE`.

## B. Perturbative one-loop spectral action

Van Nuland and van Suijlekom establish generalized one-loop renormalizability inside the spectral framework. The path integral is over finite matrix fluctuations around a fixed noncommutative gauge background, with Ward identities preserving the higher Yang-Mills/Chern-Simons spectral form of counterterms.

The current authoritative synthesis explicitly presents this as one of the **first steps** toward a quantum theory of noncommutative geometry and states that general power counting and diagrammatics beyond one loop remain undeveloped. The construction therefore supplies a controlled perturbative quantum gauge/spectral sector, not a complete fluctuating 4D Lorentzian gravitational spectral geometry.

Classification:

`PASS_SCOPED_ONE_LOOP_SPECTRAL_QUANTIZATION__BLOCKED_FULL_GRAVITY_GEOMETRY_AND_BEYOND_ONE_LOOP_COMPLETION`.

## C. 2026 nonlocal curvature-form-factor preprint — scoped watch candidate

A 2026 independent preprint computes one-loop curvature-squared form factors for the spectral action with Standard Model field content and derives a modified Newtonian potential with form-factor-dependent effective scales. This is potentially useful as a **scoped effective-gravity observable candidate** because it gives an explicit curvature-sector quantum correction.

It does not presently close the NCG family object because:

1. it is a preprint and not yet an established family authority;
2. the loop correction is generated from Standard Model field content in the spectral-action effective framework, not from a demonstrated quantum measure over the full gravitational spectral geometry;
3. the physical scale still depends on the spectral cutoff `Lambda`, and the scalar sector depends on the nonminimal coupling `xi`;
4. a same-realization ancestry from a quantum spectral geometry to those physical parameters, together with a complete GR/EFT comparator quotient and uncertainty ledger, is not supplied by the preprint alone.

Disposition:

`WATCH_SCOPED_ONE_LOOP_CURVATURE_FORM_FACTOR_OBSERVABLE__NO_FAMILY_PROMOTION`.

## D. BV / finite matrix quantization

BV and older finite spectral-geometry path-integral constructions establish that finite noncommutative geometries can be quantized consistently and that Dirac operators can serve as integration variables. These are retained as structural evidence but do not supply the missing continuum 4D Lorentzian Einstein/gravity observable chain.
