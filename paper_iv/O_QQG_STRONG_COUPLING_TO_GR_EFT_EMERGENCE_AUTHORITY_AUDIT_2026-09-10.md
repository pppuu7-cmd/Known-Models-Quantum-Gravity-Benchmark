## Question

Does the peer-reviewed 2026 QQG cosmology already possess a controlled same-realization handover

`asymptotically-free pure QQG -> RG-driven inflation -> strong coupling -> ghost confinement/nonperturbative spectrum -> generated Einstein-Hilbert/Planck sector -> reheating/radiation -> GR EFT`,

or is the last half still a conjectural boundary condition?

## A. UV -> inflation chain is a genuine strong scoped PASS

R. Liu, J. Quintin and N. Afshordi, Phys. Rev. Lett. 136, 111501 (2026), present a concrete one-loop RG cosmology in asymptotically-free quadratic gravity. In the controlled regime the running drives quasi-de Sitter / slow-roll dynamics; with sufficiently large matter content the model reaches phenomenologically viable `n_s` and `r`. Requiring the calculation not to enter the strong-coupling regime gives the characteristic lower prediction `r` of order `0.01` in their scenario.

This remains:

`PASS_SCOPED_QQG_ASYMPTOTIC_FREEDOM_TO_INFLATIONARY_OBSERVABLE_RG_CHAIN`.

The positive status is important: the branch is not merely a formal UV action.

## B. The paper itself does not derive the strong-coupling handover

The PRL explicitly states that as inflation ends the flow approaches strong coupling and that GR must emerge as an EFT so that reheating and the standard radiation era can occur. It describes the reheating surface as a possible UV-IR matching surface.

However the same paper also states that nonperturbative effects should alter the dynamics once strong coupling is entered, and lists analysis of the strong-coupling window, reheating and the EFT handover to GR as future work. It further calls for higher-loop RG calculations to quantify scheme/threshold uncertainties as GR effectively emerges.

Thus `GR must emerge` functions as a physical consistency requirement / scenario completion condition, not as an already computed nonperturbative matching theorem.

Classification:

`BLOCKED_NONPERTURBATIVE_STRONG_COUPLING_HANDOVER_TO_GR_EFT`.

## C. Holdom–Ren provides the conceptual confinement ancestry, not a closure theorem

Holdom and Ren, Phys. Rev. D 93, 124030 (2016), develop the QCD analogy for strongly coupled quadratic gravity: the strong scale may set the Planck mass, the spin-2 ghost is conjectured to be absent from the physical spectrum, and GR may emerge in the infrared.

This is a serious structural proposal and is the natural conceptual ancestor of the 2026 cosmological handover. But its own language is conjectural and analogy-driven; it does not provide a controlled nonperturbative calculation of the full QQG spectrum, ghost confinement, induced Einstein-Hilbert coefficient and matching uncertainties.

Classification:

`PASS_SCOPED_STRONG_COUPLING_QCD_ANALOGY_AND_CONFINEMENT_MECHANISM_PROPOSAL__NOT_TERMINAL_EMERGENCE_AUTHORITY`.

## D. 2025 scale-generation proposal is an independent positive control, not same-realization completion

T. de Boer, J. Kubo, M. Lindner and M. Reinig, arXiv:2510.12882, propose dynamical Planck-mass generation in scale-invariant gravity leading to Einstein gravity, inflation and Standard-Model hierarchy phenomenology.

This supports the broader plausibility that a dimensionless/higher-derivative gravity theory can dynamically generate the Einstein scale. But it is a separate realization/programme and remains an arXiv preprint in the audited record. It cannot be inserted into the Liu branch without an explicit equivalence/reduction map.

Classification:

`PROVISIONAL_SCOPED_DYNAMICAL_PLANCK_SCALE_POSITIVE_CONTROL__NO_SAME_REALIZATION_SPLICE`.

## E. June-2026 nonperturbative GR-limit result is important but solves a different link

G. Antoniou, L. Gualtieri and P. Pani, Phys. Rev. D 113, 124068 (2026), study gravitational-wave emission in quadratic gravity and show that observable deviations from GR are exponentially suppressed in the GR limit. They describe this as a nonperturbative realization of the expected equivalence between quadratic gravity and GR in the EFT limit.

This is a valuable same-family low-energy comparator/decoupling control:

`PASS_SCOPED_NONPERTURBATIVE_SUPPRESSION_OF_BEYOND_GR_OBSERVABLES_IN_GR_LIMIT`.

But it does not start from the strongly coupled pure-QQG cosmological phase and dynamically derive the Einstein-Hilbert term/Planck scale. It assumes a quadratic-gravity realization with a GR limit and tests how extra-mode signals decouple there.

Therefore it cannot close the missing strong-coupling ancestry by itself.

## F. 2026 Oda ghost-confinement programme directly attacks one missing mechanism

I. Oda, arXiv:2605.01966, formulates a BRST-quartet confinement mechanism in covariantly quantized quadratic gravity: **if** a bound state between the massive ghost and a Faddeev-Popov ghost exists, the massive ghost is confined into zero-norm states and physical-S-matrix unitarity can be restored.

The August follow-up, arXiv:2608.26085, develops a manifestly covariant/local canonical operator description, an effective asymptotic Lagrangian, dipole-field structure and multipole-state Fock space.

These are the closest 2026 works located to a concrete realization of the confinement side of the Holdom-Ren picture.

However:

1. the key bound-state/confinement condition is not yet derived from a solved strongly coupled QQG dynamics;
2. neither paper computes the cosmological RG trajectory through the transition;
3. they do not derive the generated Einstein-Hilbert coefficient / `M_Pl` and reheating map from the same state;
4. both central works are preprints in the audited record as of 2026-09-10.

Classification:

`PROVISIONAL_STRONG_GHOST_CONFINEMENT_MECHANISM_CONTROL__BOUND_STATE_DYNAMICS_AND_GR_EMERGENCE_NOT_DERIVED`.

## G. What can and cannot be combined

The following facts jointly make the strong-coupling scenario increasingly credible:

- peer-reviewed asymptotically-free RG-to-inflation dynamics;
- a long-standing QCD/confinement mechanism proposal;
- a separate dynamical-Planck-scale proposal;
- peer-reviewed nonperturbative suppression of beyond-GR signals in a GR limit;
- new BRST-quartet ghost-confinement constructions.

But RQIR same-realization discipline forbids replacing the missing transition with a collage of these separate results.

The unclosed chain remains:

`Liu UV trajectory -> approach to lambda_tH ~ O(1) -> nonperturbative spectrum/confinement -> generated positive M_Pl^2 Einstein-Hilbert sector -> controlled reheating -> low-energy GR/EFT parameters -> observable transport and uncertainty`.
