## Accepted 2025–2026 CFS progress

Causal Fermion Systems (CFS) now has several strong positive controls that retire stale blockers.

### Quantum/Fock dynamics

Dappiaggi–Finster–Kamran–Reintjes, *Holographic Mixing and Fock Space Dynamics of Causal Fermion Systems* (Ann. Henri Poincaré 27, 1885–1969, 2026), shows that in a well-defined limiting case of CFS describing Minkowski space, the causal-action dynamics together with matter can be represented as unitary evolution on fermionic and bosonic Fock spaces, reproducing linear QED-type dynamics with controlled error scaling.

The 2026 continuation arXiv:2607.08488 extends the limiting Fock-space construction to perturbative quantum field theory with non-Abelian gauge and Dirac fields.

### Relational/non-continuum ontology

Fischer–Paganini (CQG 43, 045005, 2026; arXiv:2504.19272) develops spacetime as the web of correlations of a many-body quantum system and emphasizes a relational CFS description that is not conceptually dependent on taking the continuum limit.

### Gravity continuum authority

Fischer–Finster, arXiv:2605.30199, constructs curved-spacetime CFSs from AQFT/Hadamard data with chart-independent UV regularization and finds the causal-action EL equations iff the coupled Einstein–Dirac equations hold in the analysed continuum setup.

## Frozen-RQIR interpretation

The following old blockers are obsolete:

- `NO_QUANTUM_FOCK_DYNAMICS`;
- `NO_RELATIONAL_NONCONTINUUM_DESCRIPTION`;
- `NO_CURVED_SPACETIME_CONTINUUM_GRAVITY_MAP`.

However, these achievements occupy different controlled sectors. They do not yet supply one gravity-specific **beyond-continuum** observable that can be compared in the identical physical domain to Einstein–Dirac/QFT and alternative-QG frameworks.

The current CFS research programme itself identifies direct gravity without the continuum limit and the existence of a complete set of observables as open problems.

## Reopen requirement

Provide one observable which is

1. derived directly from the causal action / non-continuum CFS dynamics rather than inserted from the continuum effective theory;
2. normalized and state/regularization specified;
3. genuinely gravity-specific rather than only QED/gauge-field Fock dynamics;
4. relational, detector-facing, asymptotic, correlation/entropy, or hard-transition interpretable;
5. computed with an identical-domain Einstein–Dirac/QFT comparator and, where relevant, LQG/AS/string comparators;
6. tested for whether the apparent difference survives ordinary effective/composite/medium descriptions.
