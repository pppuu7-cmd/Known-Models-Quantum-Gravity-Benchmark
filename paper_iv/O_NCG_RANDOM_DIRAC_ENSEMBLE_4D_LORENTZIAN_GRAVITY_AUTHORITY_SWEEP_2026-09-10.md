Finite fuzzy spectral triples admit a mathematically well-defined matrix integral over Dirac operators, with the Dirac operator serving as the geometric integration variable. This is a genuine quantum-geometric ingredient and is stronger than a merely classical spectral action.

## Decisive scope distinction

The current literature separates into two levels:

1. **action-level 4D Lorentzian availability** — explicit finite fuzzy spectral actions can be written;
2. **solved/controlled dynamical results** — the strongest detailed phase, bootstrap and matter-coupled results remain concentrated in finite and especially low-dimensional/type `(0,1)` or related Dirac ensembles.

A 4D Lorentzian action formula does not by itself establish a continuum limit, a macroscopic Lorentzian manifold, an Einstein phase, or a detector-facing gravitational observable.
