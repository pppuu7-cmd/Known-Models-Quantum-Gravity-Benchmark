## G2 — unmapped generalized GFTs

A generic GFT can be defined with field/combinatorial/RG data without establishing a gravitational interpretation. Therefore the absence of an LQG/spinfoam reduction map is not sufficient to create a new independent quantum-gravity family.

For D2 admission an unmapped generalized GFT must first provide a microscopic gravity-ancestry certificate identifying at least:

- geometric/group variables and their physical interpretation;
- gauge/closure constraints;
- simplicity or an equivalent mechanism selecting gravitational rather than topological/generic tensor degrees of freedom;
- microscopic amplitudes/dynamics and the target continuum gravitational sector;
- parameter/normalization conventions needed for same-realization transport.

Without such an object its status is:

`BLOCKED_GRAVITY_ANCESTRY__GENERIC_GFT_NOT_INDEPENDENTLY_ADMITTED_TO_TIER1`

This is an admission rule, not a scientific no-go.

`microscopic gravity TGFT -> full RG trajectory / controlled continuum limit -> condensate/relational state -> normalized gravitational observable -> GR/EFT + alternative-QG comparator -> complete error ledger`.
