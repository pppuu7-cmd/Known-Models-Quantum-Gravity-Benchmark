# Major QG Programs — Hard-Observable Audit

**Status:** frozen scoped benchmark audit / not a school-wide falsification.  
**Purpose:** close underrepresented comparator/evidence coverage for major quantum-gravity programs using the same fail-closed rule as the rest of KMQGB.

The question is deliberately narrow:

> Does the audited modern realization provide a sufficiently complete physical Lorentzian/hard graviton observable that can enter the current KMQGB comparator/COR pipeline?

Failure to provide that object is `BLOCKED_MISSING_REQUIRED_OBJECT`, not falsification of the research program.

## MGP-01 — 4D Lorentzian covariant LQG / spin foams

Modern authority: M. Han, *Ultraviolet Fixed Point in Covariant Loop Quantum Gravity*, Phys. Rev. D 114, 044040 (2026).

Retained positive result:

- complete covariant LQG amplitude sums over two-complexes;
- a candidate UV small-spin fixed point is identified;
- leading dynamics reduce to a topological theory at the fixed point;
- infinite triangulation ambiguities reduce to a finite set of boundary coefficients;
- this is a strong continuum/rigidity precedent.

KMQGB gate:

`STRONG_RIGIDITY_CONTINUUM_PRECEDENT__BLOCKED_SAME_REALIZATION_HARD_GRAVITON_CTP_OBJECT`.

Older spin-foam graviton-propagator calculations remain useful semiclassical evidence but do not replace the missing current same-realization physical 4-point/CTP hard object.

No LQG school-wide FAIL is inferred.

No GFT/TGFT school-wide FAIL is inferred.

## MGP-03 — random tensor models

Modern authority: Castro, Eichhorn, Gurau, JHEP 05 (2026) 117.

Retained positive result:

- order-4 tensor models provide a quantitative pregeometric RG study for 4D Euclidean dynamical triangulations;
- candidate fixed points/universality classes can be computed and compared with the Reuter fixed point.

KMQGB gate:

`EUCLIDEAN_PREGEOMETRIC_RG_PROGRESS__BLOCKED_LORENTZIAN_PHYSICAL_SPIN2_OBSERVABLE`.

The specific study itself finds that the simple tensor-model fixed point most likely lies in a different universality class from the Reuter fixed point. This is a scoped model-comparison result, not a falsification of random tensor models as a whole.

The audited modern objects do not yet provide the same-realization hard graviton 4-point/CTP parent required by the present KG promotion pipeline.

No causal-fermion-system school-wide FAIL is inferred.

## MGP-05 — QFT vector model / induced gravity on triangulated Lorentzian spacetime

Modern authority: Raasakka (2026), *Emergence of gravity from quantum field theory in triangulated spacetime and the QFT vector model*.

Retained positive result:

- in a concrete free massive scalar theory on two-dimensional Lorentzian triangulated spacetime, an effective gravitational action emerges from QFT amplitudes;
- this is an explicit Lorentzian induced-gravity precedent.

KMQGB gate:

`2D_LORENTZIAN_INDUCED_GRAVITY_PRECEDENT__BLOCKED_4D_QUANTUM_SPIN2_HARD_OBSERVABLE`.

Generalization to higher dimensions/other models is proposed but the concrete 4D spin-2 object required here is not supplied by the audited realization.
