## 1. Why the comparator must be upgraded

An outdated red-team caricature would say that spinfoam/LQG constructions necessarily retain uncontrolled infinite triangulation dependence and therefore fail the finite-parent-data requirement before any hard observable is considered.

The 2026 results make that shortcut unsafe.

Han considers the complete covariant-LQG amplitude obtained by summing spinfoam amplitudes over two-complexes using spin-network/spinfoam stacks. The August 2026 paper identifies a **candidate** UV fixed point in the small-spin regime. At that fixed point the complete amplitude reduces at leading order to a topological theory, while the infinite triangulation ambiguities reduce to a finite set of boundary coefficients associated with a finite basis of three-dimensional boundary blocks.

The April 2026 companion result gives a controlled limit in which spinfoam-stack amplitudes localize onto SU(2) flat connections; on topologically trivial manifolds the amplitude factorizes into a triangulation-dependent normalization and a finite boundary-data part, and a renormalized amplitude becomes independent of the bulk two-complex structure in that limit.
