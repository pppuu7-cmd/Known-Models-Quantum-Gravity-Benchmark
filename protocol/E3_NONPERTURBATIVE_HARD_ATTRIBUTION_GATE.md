# E3 Nonperturbative Hard Gravity-Attribution Gate

**Status:** permanent O1/P4 search gate.  
**KMQGB iteration:** 071.  
**Purpose:** prevent a nonperturbative effect in an ordinary matter/QFT sector from being mislabeled as a nonperturbative quantum-gravity parent merely because it appears in a graviton amplitude.

## 1. Core distinction

A four-graviton or gravitational response observable can contain nonperturbative physics without that nonperturbative physics being intrinsic to quantum gravity.

Schematically,

`ordinary quantum sector --coupled through T_munu--> graviton observable`

may generate exponentially small terms, condensates, bound-state thresholds, instantons or strong-coupling spectral structures.

Those are important quantum effects, but KMQGB must attribute their origin before assigning them to Candidate Gravity.

## 2. Positive control demonstrating the issue

Bern, Herrmann, Kosmopoulos and Roiban, *Effective Field Theory Islands from Perturbative and Nonperturbative Four-Graviton Amplitudes* (2022), give explicit perturbative and nonperturbative `2->2` graviton amplitudes. Their first nonperturbative example is a four-dimensional `N=1` supersymmetric field theory weakly coupled to gravity.

The resulting low-energy gravitational EFT data lie on the same small coefficient islands highlighted by perturbative string and massive-matter loop examples.

This is a strong control showing that

`nonperturbative + four-graviton`

does not imply

`intrinsic quantum-gravity residual`.

## 3. E3 gravity-attribution requirement

Before an O1/E3 candidate can enter P4, identify the origin of the nonperturbative weight/phase.

At minimum distinguish:

### NP-M — matter/ordinary-QFT nonperturbativity

Examples:

- Yang-Mills/QCD-like strong dynamics;
- ordinary gauge instantons;
- condensates/bound states in a non-gravitational sector;
- supersymmetric matter/gauge strong coupling;
- hidden-sector transseries coupled through ordinary stress energy.

Default comparator classification:

`C4/C6/known-quantum-sector + gravitational coupling`.

Not KG-specific without an additional independent gravitational relation.

### NP-G — intrinsic gravitational-parent nonperturbativity

Candidate examples in principle:

- a gravitational saddle whose action and measure are fixed by the same parent that gives the perturbative graviton sector;
- a nonperturbative completion of the gravitational constraint/path-integral problem with a Lorentzian observable map;
- a gravity-specific spectral transition not reproducible by ordinary quantum sources/mediators;
- another parent-fixed sector that changes a hard graviton/CTP relation even after all ordinary quantum sectors are profiled.

Only NP-G is eligible for E3 P4 consideration.

## 4. Required E3 parent data

An intrinsic E3 proposal must prospectively provide

1. the parent object/action/constraint/integral defining the relevant saddle/sector;
2. the nonperturbative action `S_np(theta)` rather than an inserted `exp(-S)`;
3. the prefactor/phase/Stokes data needed for the physical Lorentzian observable;
4. the rule selecting the relevant saddle/thimble/sector;
5. the hard gravitational tensor/helicity observable affected;
6. the same-parent retarded/CTP continuation;
7. a statement of which ordinary matter/source nonperturbative sectors are held fixed as comparators;
8. a common-domain proof that the effect is not absorbable into C4/C5/C6/state/topology nuisance freedom.

If any required nonperturbative weight or Stokes constant is an arbitrary independent function/parameter tower, apply `P4_FUNCTIONAL_FREEDOM_NO_GO` as well.

## 5. Hard-observable requirement

Quantum-cosmology wave functions, Euclidean partition functions, entropy corrections or saddle classifications do not by themselves satisfy the current P4 task.

The current task requires an explicit relation in a hard/cross-representation gravitational observable.

For an E3 candidate, a minimal acceptable bridge has the schematic form

`parent saddle/sector -> Delta A_4^np(s,t,u;helicities)`

and/or

`parent saddle/sector -> Delta G_R / Delta Gamma_CTP`

with shared nonperturbative data.

A cosmological or Euclidean result may be supporting evidence only until this Lorentzian observable bridge is derived.

## 6. Resurgence is a method, not a selector by itself

A transseries

`F(g)=sum_n a_n g^n + sigma exp(-S/g) sum_n b_n g^n + ...`

can encode strong nonperturbative rigidity, but KMQGB must ask what fixes

- `S`;
- the transseries parameter `sigma`;
- Stokes constants;
- the physical summation prescription;
- the observable continuation.

If these data are supplied by a known string/matrix/matter comparator, A3 fails. If they are left free, A2/A1 fails. Resurgence alone therefore does not constitute P4.

## 7. Current literature boundary

Current audited examples separate into three groups:

- Lorentzian graviton spectral RG: nonperturbative and gravity-specific but registered asymptotic-safety comparator, with complete hard scattering hierarchy still incomplete;
- Lorentzian quantum-cosmology resurgence: nonperturbative saddle/thimble information exists, but not the required four-graviton/CTP hard parent;
- nonperturbative ordinary field theories weakly coupled to gravity: explicit four-graviton data exist, but gravity attribution fails because the nonperturbative source is an ordinary quantum sector.

Thus O1 remains open but significantly narrower.

## 8. O1 promotion test

Classify an O1 search hit as `E3_HARD_NEAR_SURVIVOR` only if all hold:

- intrinsic NP-G origin is explicit;
- parent data are finite/rigid;
- Lorentzian hard graviton or CTP relation is derived;
- ordinary matter/source nonperturbative comparators cannot reproduce it;
- string/matrix/asymptotic-safety/discrete/nonlocal comparator containment is not immediate.

Otherwise preserve it as comparator/supporting nonperturbative evidence.

## 9. Score consequence

This gate sharpens O1 but is not itself a P4 survivor. R1/R2/R3/R4 remain unchanged under frozen rubrics.
