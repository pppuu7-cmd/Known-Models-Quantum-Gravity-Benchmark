## A. Beyond-C5 escape declaration

Any future exploratory KG record must state how it exits the local analytic full-C5 class. Allowed structural doors are tracked as

- E1 nonlocal/nonanalytic same-spin-2 dynamics;
- E2 extra propagating DOF/mediator;
- E3 genuinely nonperturbative/transseries sector;
- E4 modified fundamental quantum dynamics;
- E5 modified symmetry/causal/microstructure.

A seed that cannot identify an escape door is comparator-contained by construction.

## C. Causality correction to E1

A cubic-curvature seed that changes the graviton three-point coupling carries a high-energy causality tax in the weakly coupled CEMZ regime. A minimal no-extra-tower search therefore moves the **candidate-specific** first departure to four-point/higher order unless a UV completion explicitly explains the escape.

This is a search-priority rule, not a theorem that all cubic structures vanish.

## D. Quartic-first rigidity object

At tree EFT bookkeeping level in 4D GREFT, define

`f2=C4,1-C4,2`,

`g2=C4,1+C4,2`,

`rho4=f2/g2`.

In the scoped local/polynomial positivity class, `-1<rho4<1`.

`rho4` is an IR anchor, not KG novelty. A serious parent must predict a linked tower of helicity/crossing coefficients `a_(k,j)` from few shared parameters and survive holdout across helicity/order/energy/angle blocks.

## E. Spectral-moment rigidity and its limit

For suitable parity-preserving local dispersive graviton eigenchannels, EFT coefficients form moments of a positive-semidefinite spectral-density matrix and associated Hankel matrices are positive semidefinite.

Use moment/Hankel constraints as cross-order consistency diagnostics.

Do **not** interpret low-rank or saturated Hankel structure as KG-specific: simple ordinary spectral support can generate it.

## G. Kinematic projection-rank completeness

If parent response kinematics have dimension `n` and the target/native observable has dimension `m<n`, a unique local pullback requires `n-m` additional independent same-parent constraints or an equivalent prospectively frozen lift `x=gamma(s)`.

Required rank condition:

`rank([J_target; J_aux]) = n`.

Without it, target derivatives/discontinuities/root Jacobians/residuals are not uniquely defined and downstream comparator/COR is blocked.

External RQIR Iter611 is the motivating example: three independent source invariants map to one native hard variable, so two auxiliary constraints are required.

## I. Radiative protection / full-C5 three-point rule

Pure quantum Einstein gravity generates a nonzero two-loop cubic-curvature Goroff-Sagnotti counterterm.

Therefore literal all-orders `C3=0` is **not** the correct generic quartic-first rule.

Use instead:

`full matched C5 through 3-point + first candidate-specific independent deformation at 4-point/higher`.

Any stronger absence/fixed relation in the cubic sector must be protected by

- exact symmetry/selection rule;

- nonperturbative identity;

Radiative/operator-mixing closure is part of response completeness.

## J. Parent-principle audit

A kernel-selection principle must **derive** a hierarchy, not merely constrain one.

Current classification:

1. **S-matrix bootstrap / crossing-unitarity-Regge-softness:** strong rigidity precedent, but string/string-like amplitudes are direct comparators.
2. **supersymmetry/duality/protected hierarchy:** strong protection precedent, but substantial extra structure/spectrum and known comparator overlap.
3. **nonperturbative RG/fixed-point dynamics:** can generate momentum-dependent 3/4-graviton vertices, but asymptotic safety is already a comparator class.
4. **causal/discrete microstructure:** promising way to derive nonlocal kinematics, but no complete stable quantum spin-2 CTP/four-graviton parent yet for this benchmark.
5. **positivity/spectral density/ghost freedom alone:** consistency constraints, not a unique kernel-selection principle.

Therefore a future KG parent principle must satisfy both

`RIGIDITY`: finite/low-dimensional tensor + momentum hierarchy fixed before data;

`NOVELTY`: common-domain relation outside full C5, string-like/dual-resonance, asymptotic-safety and other comparator manifolds.
