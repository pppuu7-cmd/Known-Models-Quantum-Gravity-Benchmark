## 1. Why this matters

This proves, again, that

`finite seed -> infinite all-point hierarchy`

is mathematically achievable.

## 2. Finite generator

In Eynard-Orantin/Chekhov-Eynard-Orantin recursion, the unstable initial data (spectral curve / one- and two-point seed objects and branch structure) determine the stable multidifferentials recursively.

Thus no independent function needs to be chosen separately at every `(g,n)` once the spectral curve is frozen.

For the internal architecture this is an A2 positive control:

`A2 PASS_AS_RECURSIVE_GENERATOR_GIVEN_FIXED_SPECTRAL_CURVE`.

The important caveat is that the spectral curve itself is parent data and must have a physical origin.

## 5. Design lesson

The desirable feature to copy is **generator completeness**, not the spectral-curve architecture.

A successful new parent should have a finite seed and a recursion/evolution law for connected observables such that

- the seed has an independent gravity-specific physical derivation;
- no independent higher-point functions enter;
- the generator respects Ward/factorization/causality rather than adding them post hoc;
- the resulting hard hierarchy is not a known matrix/string/spinfoam/AS/nonlocal comparator.

Thus the search target is now a **gravity-native all-point generator**.
