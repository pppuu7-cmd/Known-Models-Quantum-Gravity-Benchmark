# Matrix-Model Microscopic Parent Precedent

**Status:** frozen comparator/design precedent / no KG promotion.  
**Purpose:** establish a high standard for what counts as an actual parent principle: a microscopic action/Hamiltonian that produces concrete gravitational observables without inserting an arbitrary form factor by hand.

## MM-01 — BFSS as microscopic M-theory parent

The BFSS matrix quantum mechanics is a concrete microscopic Hamiltonian proposed as a nonperturbative definition of M-theory.

Recent 2026 progress now includes direct gravitational observable checks rather than only indirect duality arguments:

- supersymmetric localization of BFSS with scattering boundary conditions reproduces the expected momentum dependence of the M-theory three-graviton amplitude;
- a separate 2026 construction obtains a class of n-point single-minus graviton amplitudes from BFSS through a BPS index / wall-crossing problem and matches gravity in the controlled kinematic regime;
- higher-point BFSS correlators are also being developed as simulation targets.

KMQGB classification:

`STRONG_MICROSCOPIC_PARENT_PRECEDENT__M_THEORY_COMPARATOR`.

This satisfies the **rigidity/derivation** side of the P4 standard much better than a phenomenological form-factor ansatz, but it fails the **novelty** side for Candidate Gravity because it is a known M/string-theory architecture.

## MM-02 — IKKT / type-IIB matrix model

The IKKT matrix action provides another parameter-economical microscopic construction. On suitable 3+1-dimensional quantum-spacetime branes with fuzzy compact factors, one-loop dynamics generate an Einstein-Hilbert term and an effective Newton constant. Modern work also derives higher-spin-extended and large-scale modified-gravity sectors, and Monte-Carlo/localization studies probe emergent spacetime vacua.

KMQGB classification:

`STRONG_EMERGENT_GRAVITY_PARENT_PRECEDENT__EXTRA_MATRIX_FUZZY_HIGHER_SPIN_STRUCTURE`.

The audited realization is not a minimal metric-only KG candidate: its effective gravity depends on matrix degrees of freedom, compact fuzzy sectors and associated additional modes/structures.

## MM-03 — hard-observable standard

Matrix models teach a permanent methodological lesson:

A serious parent principle should expose an explicit chain such as

`microscopic action/Hamiltonian`

`-> states/boundary data`

`-> gravitational amplitude/correlator`

`-> low-energy GR limit`

`-> higher-point/cross-regime predictions`.

A proposal that merely states a symmetry, positivity condition, spectral representation or preferred nonlocal function without such a chain does not meet the same derivational standard.

## MM-04 — comparator consequence

Future KG candidates with matrix/algebraic microscopic origins must be compared against

- BFSS/M-theory amplitudes;
- IKKT/type-IIB matrix-model emergent gravity;
- string/dual-resonance amplitude structure;
- holographic/matrix-correlator limits where a common observable exists.

Using `matrix emergence` as a qualitative novelty claim is insufficient.

## MM-05 — P4 consequence

The constructive P4 target is sharpened to:

> find an explicit low-freedom microscopic or equally derivational parent rule that produces a concrete hard gravitational relation and is **not merely a point/rephrasing of known BFSS/IKKT/string/holographic comparator space**.

No such new object is established by this audit. R4 therefore does not increase.
