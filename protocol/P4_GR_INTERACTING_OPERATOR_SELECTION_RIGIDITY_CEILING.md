## 1. Two complementary rigidity controls

There are two closely related but logically distinct classical consistency results relevant to the Iter132–137 operator-selection problem.

### 1.1 Four-dimensional Lovelock-type rigidity

Under the standard four-dimensional metric assumptions, requiring the gravitational field equations to be built locally from the metric and its derivatives up to second order, to be diffeomorphism covariant and divergence free, selects the Einstein tensor plus the cosmological metric term.

Schematically,

`local metric-only + <=2 derivatives + diffeo covariance + divergence-free -> G_mn + Lambda g_mn`.

In four dimensions the nontrivial higher Lovelock densities do not add independent local second-order metric dynamics.

### 1.2 Consistent massless-spin-2 self-coupling

Starting from the free Pauli-Fierz massless spin-2 field, consistency of local nonlinear self-coupling and gauge symmetry strongly drives the theory to the generally covariant Einstein interaction under the standard assumptions. BRST/cohomological analyses also impose strong no-go restrictions on cross-interactions among multiple positive-energy massless spin-2 fields.

Thus

`one ordinary massless spin-2 + local consistent gauge deformation/self-coupling -> Einstein-type nonlinear gravity control`.

These are positive rigidity results, not merely exclusions.

## 3. GR rigidity corridor

A candidate remains inside the GR rigidity corridor if it simultaneously assumes, in the relevant hard domain,

1. a single ordinary massless spin-2 gravitational field;
2. standard local field-theory composition;
3. ordinary diffeomorphism / Pauli-Fierz gauge deformation;
4. local metric dynamics with at most the derivative structure covered by the rigidity theorem being invoked;
5. no additional propagating states or nonlocal singular structure;
6. standard unitary/factorization law.

Within that corridor a claimed new operator must first explain why it is not simply a field redefinition, local higher-order EFT term, gauge artifact or ordinary GR completion.
