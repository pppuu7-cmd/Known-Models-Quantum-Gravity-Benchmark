# E2 Fresh Gravitational-Completeness Pressure — 2026

**Status:** scoped E2 comparator/consistency upgrade / no P4 credit.  
**KMQGB iteration:** 098.  
**Primary reference:** *Completeness from gravitational scattering*, Phys. Rev. D 113, 106008 (published 8 May 2026).

## 1. Why this matters

The same-spectrum search through Iter097 is exceptionally narrow, so one possible escape is E2: introduce a new particle/state sector that changes factorization poles, residues or unitarity cuts.

A naive minimality strategy would be to add one or a few new charged states and assume the extension remains finite.

Fresh gravitational-scattering consistency results show that this assumption can fail sharply.

## 2. Scoped theorem/control

Under the assumptions stated in the cited work, including

1. a weakly coupled ultraviolet completion of gravity;
2. a non-Abelian symmetry `G` whose Cartan subgroup generates an Abelian charge lattice;
3. a finite initial set of charged particle representations,

consistency of perturbative gravitational scattering implies a completeness statement: infinitely many charged particles are required, populating the relevant charge lattice under the conditions analyzed.

This is a strong **scoped** control. KMQGB does not extrapolate it to every possible state, symmetry, strong-coupling regime or gravity theory.

## 3. E2 consequence

A future candidate that introduces a finite charged sector must check whether its symmetry/UV assumptions trigger a gravitational-completeness mechanism.

If they do, the true parent spectrum is not the finite seed but an infinite tower/lattice, and the candidate must

- derive that tower and its couplings;
- include it in hard factorization/unitarity sums;
- profile it against string, KK, higher-spin, gauge-completion and other tower comparators;
- keep A2 finite at the **parent-law** level rather than counting infinitely many derived states as independent parameters.

## 4. Important distinction

An infinite **predicted** tower is not an A2 failure if all masses/charges/couplings follow from finite parent data.

The consistency pressure does, however, invalidate a false minimality claim in which only one charged state is retained while the parent assumptions require the rest of the lattice.

## 5. Search consequence

E2 remains open, but any finite charged extension is now screened by this completeness test before detailed P4 development.
