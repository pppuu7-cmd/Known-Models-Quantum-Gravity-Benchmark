# Kernel-Selection Mechanism Audit

**Status:** frozen P4 red-team audit / no Candidate Gravity promotion.  
**Purpose:** test several mechanisms that appear capable of removing arbitrary functional freedom from higher-curvature/nonlocal gravity and determine whether any supplies a novel KG parent principle.

## K1 — cutoff spectral action

**Classification:** `RIGID_RELATIVE_COEFFICIENTS__REMAINS_FUNCTIONAL_INPUT_AND_KNOWN_ARCHITECTURE`.

The spectral-action construction derives correlated higher-curvature coefficients from a spectral triple and heat-kernel expansion, greatly reducing independent Wilson freedom.

However the conventional cutoff spectral action still contains

- the spectral triple/Dirac operator;
- a scale `Lambda`;
- a positive cutoff function `chi`, whose moments feed the derivative expansion.

Thus the mechanism is a strong rigidity precedent but not a unique kernel selection. It is also an established noncommutative-geometry architecture and must be treated as a comparator, not a new KG principle.

## K2 — zeta spectral action

**Classification:** `REMOVES_CUTOFF_FUNCTION__KNOWN_LOCAL_SPECTRAL_GEOMETRY_COMPARATOR`.

Zeta-function regularization removes the arbitrary cutoff function from the bosonic spectral action and yields a local finite action controlled by spectral/Dirac data and dimensionful physical inputs.

This is a stronger selection mechanism than the cutoff action. But for KMQGB it does not currently provide

- a novel quartic/higher cross-regime graviton kernel;
- the required quantum spin-2 CTP/relational parent;
- comparator-orthogonal separation from known spectral/noncommutative-geometry and local EFT structures.

Therefore it is a useful precedent that functional freedom can be reduced by a geometric definition, not a P4 survivor.

## K3 — covariant diffusion / semigroup exponential

**Classification:** `EXPONENTIAL_OF_GENERATOR_SELECTED__GENERATOR_AND_CAUSALITY_NOT_SELECTED`.

A one-parameter semigroup satisfying composition and continuity has the form

`U(tau)=exp(tau L)`

for its generator `L` under standard assumptions.

This can motivate exponential form factors when `L` is chosen as a covariant differential operator.

But the semigroup principle does not by itself choose the generator `L`, its tensor placement or Lorentzian boundary prescription. Choosing `L=Box/M^2` is an additional physical assumption and lands in known entire/nonlocal form-factor classes.

Moreover standard entire nonlocal retarded functions can develop spacelike/advanced tails; exponential form alone is not a causality theorem.

Thus semigroup composition removes one layer of functional freedom but does not close the KG parent-selection problem.

## K4 — retarded Stieltjes / positive spectral kernel

**Classification:** `CAUSAL_SPECTRAL_CLASS__ARBITRARY_SPECTRAL_DENSITY_REMAINS`.

A retarded Stieltjes-type kernel can encode causal memory through a positive spectral density and has attractive stability/dispersion properties in scoped constructions.

But the class is parameterized by the spectral density `rho(mu)` (or a family of its moments/scales). Positivity and retarded support constrain `rho`; they do not generally select a unique density.

Hence Stieltjes representation is a good causality/spectral **parameterization** and cross-background test language, not yet a low-freedom parent principle.

## K5 — minimal spectral support

**Classification:** `LOW_RANK_SPECTRAL_MINIMALITY__EXTRA_STATE_OR_CONTINUUM_COMPARATOR`.

Imposing the smallest possible positive spectral support can reduce moment/Hankel rank. But

- one or a few delta-function supports correspond to one or a few massive exchanged/resonant states in the relevant channel;
- a new continuum is still additional UV spectral information;
- ordinary weakly coupled heavy states or known UV completions can generate low-rank/extremal moment patterns.

Therefore spectral minimality by itself enters E2/UV-completion comparator space and is not KG-specific.

## P4 conclusion

None of K1–K5 satisfies the current P4 deliverable:

- a genuinely new physical principle;
- explicit low-freedom parent rule;
- derived quartic/higher or cross-representation relation;
- immediate structural separation from known comparator architectures.

The search remains open.

## Strong retained lesson

The distinction is now:

`parameterization reduction` ≠ `parent derivation` ≠ `comparator-orthogonal novelty`.

A future P4 survivor must close all three steps.
