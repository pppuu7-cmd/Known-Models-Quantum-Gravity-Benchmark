## 1. Postquantum classical gravity

Control:

- Oppenheim, Sajjad, *Stochastic modes in postquantum classical gravity*, arXiv:2605.05375 (2026).

This is a strong modern classical-quantum hybrid control. The theory couples classical spacetime to quantum matter through a covariant stochastic framework. Linearization around Minkowski space yields stochastic classical spin-2 and spin-0 modes; the action is positive semidefinite on the dynamical modes, and two-point/power-spectral observables can be computed and constrained.

This demonstrates that a mathematically structured gravity-linked stochastic theory can have finite parameters and concrete observables.
