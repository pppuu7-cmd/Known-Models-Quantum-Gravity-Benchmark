# S-Local Finite-Range QCA — Conditional IR C5 Containment Theorem

**Status:** permanent conditional containment result for the MISP2/QCA branch.  
**KMQGB iteration:** 067.  
**Purpose:** prevent ordinary analytic lattice corrections to GR from being mislabeled as beyond-C5 Candidate Gravity.

## 1. Conditional statement

Consider a microscopic finite-range quantum cellular automaton / finite-depth local unitary parent with lattice scale `a`.

Assume all of the following on a declared low-energy domain `Q a << 1`:

1. **S-local microscopic dynamics:** finite interaction range/depth and standard unitary closed-system quantum dynamics;
2. **isolated low-energy content:** the only gravitational gapless modes are the ordinary two massless spin-2 helicities, plus already-declared known light matter;
3. **UV separation:** other microscopic/lattice modes relevant to the reduction are separated by a nonzero scale of order `a^-1` or another declared heavy threshold;
4. **smooth continuum expansion:** amplitudes/response kernels around the chosen relativistic point are analytic in the heavy-sector momenta order by order;
5. **ordinary IR symmetry:** after physical reduction, the low-energy gravitational sector realizes the ordinary Lorentz/diffeomorphism/GR Ward structure to the order on which the candidate claim is made;
6. **no independent nonperturbative/nonlocal branch:** no additional E1/E3/E4 mechanism is invoked in the same claim.

Then the short-distance corrections generated by the finite-range UV structure admit the usual local derivative expansion in the low-energy domain.

For the metric/massless-spin-2 gravitational sector, this has the schematic form

`Gamma_IR = Gamma_EH + sum_i c_i(theta) a^(Delta_i-4) O_i[g,matter] + massless-loop nonanalytic pieces`,

where the local operators `O_i` are the ordinary symmetry-allowed higher-curvature/derivative EFT operators and the nonanalytic long-distance pieces from retained massless fields are the ordinary GR/QFT loop sector.

Therefore, on this domain,

`Gamma_IR in full matched C5`.

### Consequence

A purely analytic low-energy finite-lattice correction from an S-local QCA is **not** a beyond-C5 residual merely because its Wilson coefficients are predicted by a microscopic automaton.

The microscopic theory may be highly predictive, but its low-energy point lies inside the full-C5 theory space.

## 2. Why microscopic predictivity does not imply comparator separation

Suppose the parent fixes an infinite Wilson tower

`c_i = c_i(theta)`

from a finite parameter vector `theta`.

This is excellent A2 rigidity: infinitely many predictions need not mean infinitely many choices.

However, full C5 allows the applicable low-energy Wilson coefficients to vary over the matched EFT comparator manifold. A particular finite-dimensional relation among them defines a **submanifold of C5**, not automatically a direction outside C5.

Thus

`parent rigidity != comparator-orthogonal residual`.

If the observable vector generated by the QCA can be reproduced by one allowed C5 coefficient point on the same common domain, G5/A3 containment remains.

Cross-order relations can still be scientifically valuable for UV inference, but they are not uniquely quantum-gravitational residuals against a comparator that already permits the corresponding EFT coefficients.

## 3. Standard EFT basis logic

The result is the direct application of Wilsonian low-energy reasoning:

- integrating out heavy/gapped short-distance physics yields local operators expandable in powers of external momenta over the heavy scale;
- ordinary gravitational symmetry organizes the pure-gravity terms into curvature and covariant-derivative invariants;
- massless propagating fields can generate nonanalytic momentum dependence, but those contributions belong to the matched low-energy loop sector rather than providing a new UV-lattice signature by themselves.

Representative gravity-EFT authorities include J. F. Donoghue, *General relativity as an effective field theory: The leading quantum corrections*, Phys. Rev. D 50, 3874 (1994), and modern higher-curvature EFT analyses.

This theorem is also a direct specialization of the already frozen KMQGB baseline in `protocol/BEYOND_C5_ESCAPE_TAXONOMY.md`.

## 4. Lorentz/diffeomorphism-breaking lattice artifacts

A finite lattice can generate low-energy artifacts that do **not** respect the ordinary Lorentz/diffeomorphism structure exactly.

Such a term does not evade the theorem by magic; instead the proposal has changed escape door.

Classify it under

`E5 — modified symmetry / causal / kinematic structure`.

Then the candidate must pay the full E5 taxes:

- exact physical-DOF/constraint closure;
- recovery of observed Lorentz/diffeomorphism/GR limits;
- preferred-frame/isotropy tests;
- causal/no-signalling consistency;
- phenomenological bounds on symmetry breaking;
- comparison with existing Lorentz-violating/discrete-kinematics models.

A lattice artifact is therefore not P4 merely because it is outside covariant C5.

## 5. Extra poles or bound states

If the microscopic QCA produces additional light/near-threshold propagating or composite degrees of freedom, assumption 2 fails.

The candidate then enters

`E2 — additional propagating degrees of freedom / mediator`

and must be compared against scalar/vector/massive-spin-2/composite/continuum/tower and induced-gravity alternatives.

Again, this is an escape route with taxes, not automatic novelty.

## 6. Hard Brillouin-zone regime

At

`Q a ~ 1`,

the local derivative expansion can fail, and the exact finite-lattice symbol may contain genuinely non-polynomial Brillouin-zone structure that no finite C5 truncation reproduces.

This is the most direct QCA-specific hard regime.

However, it is outside the low-energy theorem and must satisfy a different set of conditions:

- candidate and comparator must be defined on the same high-energy domain;
- the microscopic update must remain authoritative there;
- the exact hard object must be observable/prospectively defined rather than inferred from a low-energy fit;
- full discrete-QCA/lattice/discrete-gravity comparators must be included;
- if continuum C5 is beyond its validity domain, it cannot be used as a straw comparator.

Thus a Brillouin-zone hard signature can be scientifically interesting but does not license UV-vs-IR comparator cheating.

## 7. Nonanalytic / collective exceptions

The theorem also does not apply when the parent derives a genuine low-energy effect that is not captured by the ordinary local analytic short-distance expansion, for example a controlled

- E1 parent-fixed nonlocal/nonanalytic kernel;
- E3 nonperturbative/transseries contribution;
- E4 fundamental open-system modification;
- E5 deformed causal/kinematic structure;
- collective/threshold spectral feature that must be reclassified under E2/E3 as appropriate.

Every such exception must be independently derived and pass the corresponding Beyond-C5 escape audit.
