# Composition / Gluing / Duality P4 Prefilter

**Status:** terminal constructive prefilter; negative evidence only.  
**KMQGB iteration:** 058.  
**Wave:** 38.  
**Purpose:** test several tempting low-freedom parent principles before any Candidate Gravity ansatz is promoted.

## Scope and rule

The P4 rubric requires more than a structural slogan. A survivor must derive a concrete hard 4-point/higher or cross-representation gravitational relation, with few free data, declared spectral/dispersion class, Ward/CTP/causal/radiative scope, and immediate comparator survival.

This prefilter asks whether composition, gluing, associativity, UV/IR reciprocity, or causal-diamond overlap consistency by themselves can supply that selector.

None of the five branches below survives as a novel P4 parent selector. This is useful search-space pruning but carries **no R4 credit**.

## G1 — Null-slab / causal-diamond projector gluing

**Candidate principle:** local quantum-gravitational amplitudes are obtained by gluing null-boundary Hilbert-space data with a physical projector, and consistency under causal-diamond composition selects the dynamics.

**Comparator finding:** Theofilis and Wieland, *Foundational Structure of Local Amplitudes in Quantum Gravity* (arXiv:2508.09679, 2025), formulate precisely such a theory-independent local-amplitude scaffold: null slabs, corner/bulk factors, generalized projectors, causal-diamond contractions, Ward identities and charge conservation. Their discussion explicitly notes realizations in spinfoam and group-field-theory approaches.

**Classification:** `GENERAL_QG_SCAFFOLD__PROJECTOR_NOT_SELECTED`.

**Reason:** gluing determines how a supplied physical projector is composed; it does not by itself select a unique projector or a new hard 4-point graviton kernel. A KG parent would need an additional microscopic rule fixing the projector and yielding an observable relation outside spinfoam/GFT/full-C5 comparator manifolds.

## G2 — Associator / curved composition as the gravitational primitive

**Candidate principle:** the first new gravitational interaction is the controlled failure of strict associativity of an operational composition law; the associator is identified with curvature and fixes higher interactions.

**Comparator finding:** relative-locality constructions already connect nonlinear/nonassociative momentum composition with momentum-space connection/curvature and microscopic causal structure. See Amelino-Camelia, Freidel, Kowalski-Glikman and Smolin, *Relative locality: A deepening of the relativity principle* (arXiv:1106.0313) and related curved-momentum-space literature.

**Classification:** `RELATIVE_LOCALITY_CONTAINMENT_RISK__NO_UNIQUE_HARD_GRAVITON_KERNEL`.

**Reason:** an associator can encode geometric/kinematic curvature without fixing a unique Lorentzian spin-2 scattering/CTP hierarchy. Associativity failure alone is therefore not a KG certificate.

## G3 — Nonassociative star/R-flux gravitational parent

**Candidate principle:** promote the associator to a microscopic nonassociative product and derive gravity from the deformed product.

**Comparator finding:** nonassociative star-product/R-flux gravity is already an explicit string-related modified-gravity/QG program. A modern example is S. Vacaru, *Asymptotic safe nonassociative quantum gravity with star R-flux products, Goroff–Sagnotti counter-terms, and geometric flows*, Annals of Physics 470 (2024) 169812, DOI 10.1016/j.aop.2024.169812.

**Classification:** `STRING_R_FLUX_NONASSOCIATIVE_COMPARATOR_CONTAINED`.

**Reason:** generic nonassociativity or a star-product deformation is already inside an established string/noncommutative comparator family. A viable P4 proposal would need a different microscopic origin plus an explicit hard observable not removable into this class or ordinary nonlocal/EFT freedom.

## G4 — UV/IR reciprocal spectral self-duality

**Candidate principle:** a UV/IR inversion symmetry fixes high-energy gravitational spectral data from infrared data, eliminating an arbitrary Wilson tower.

**Comparator finding:** Born reciprocity and its string/metastring implementations already make position/momentum or UV/IR reciprocity a foundational duality, closely related to generalized T-duality and Born geometry. See Freidel, Leigh and Minic, *Born reciprocity in string theory and the nature of spacetime*, Physics Letters B 730 (2014) 302–306, arXiv:1307.7080.

**Classification:** `BORN_RECIPROCITY_STRING_DUALITY_CONTAINMENT`.

**Reason:** an inversion/self-duality condition can reduce functional freedom, but without a further dynamical selector it does not uniquely determine the hard graviton spectral measure or CTP kernel. A raw self-duality ansatz is therefore insufficient for P4.

## G5 — Causal-diamond overlap-spectrum consistency

**Candidate principle:** overlapping causal diamonds must assign the shared subsystem exactly the same entanglement spectrum, and this overlap consistency selects quantum gravity dynamics.

**Comparator finding:** Holographic Space-Time/Hilbert-bundle formulations already impose a closely related quantum principle of relativity: the largest common diamond is represented as a tensor factor in each Hilbert-space fiber and must have the same entanglement spectrum when computed from either fiber dynamics. See T. Banks, *Hilbert Bundles and Holographic Space-time: the Hydrodynamic Approach to Gravity* (arXiv:2502.04924, 2025) and related HST work.

**Classification:** `HST_OVERLAP_CONSISTENCY_COMPARATOR`.

**Reason:** overlap-spectrum consistency is a meaningful microscopic consistency condition, but it is not novel relative to HST and does not by itself supply the required complete 4D Lorentzian hard spin-2 4-point/CTP relation.

## Combined lesson

The tested branches share the same failure mode:

`structural consistency principle -> many admissible dynamics`.

For KMQGB P4, the missing ingredient is not another abstract consistency relation. The next proposal must contain a **dynamical selection rule** that maps a finite microscopic object directly to a hard gravitational relation. In particular, the rule should not be reducible to any of:

- projector gluing alone;
- nonassociative/curved composition alone;
- string/noncommutative star-product deformation;
- UV/IR or Born reciprocity alone;
- overlap-spectrum consistency alone.

The strongest remaining constructive target is therefore a parent of the form

`finite microscopic dynamical object -> uniquely normalized hard 4pt/higher kernel -> same-parent CTP/retarded block -> GR infrared anchor`,

with no independent per-order Wilson/function freedom.
