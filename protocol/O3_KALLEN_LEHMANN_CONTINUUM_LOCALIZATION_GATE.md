# O3 Källén-Lehmann Continuum-Localization Gate

**Status:** permanent O3/E2 attribution gate.  
**KMQGB iteration:** 078.  
**Purpose:** show why an unusual positive two-point spectral function or branch cut is not, by itself, a Candidate Gravity novelty certificate.

## 1. Scope

Consider a physical/gauge-invariant or properly projected two-point response that admits a standard positive Källén-Lehmann representation in a unitary Lorentzian domain,

`G(p^2) = ∫_0^∞ dmu^2 rho(mu^2) / [p^2-mu^2+i0]`,

with `rho(mu^2) >= 0` up to separately handled isolated massless poles/subtractions.

For retarded response, replace the Feynman prescription by the corresponding parent-fixed retarded prescription. The spectral measure is the key object.

This statement is made for the physical projected sector; gauge-dependent propagator components need not have a positive measure.

## 2. Continuum auxiliary-field realization

Introduce a continuum of local fields `phi_mu` with action schematically

`S_aux = ∫ dmu^2 [ 1/2 phi_mu (Box-mu^2) phi_mu + g(mu) phi_mu J ]`,

where

`g(mu)^2 = rho(mu^2)`

with conventions absorbed into `g` and the measure.

Integrating out the Gaussian continuum gives

`S_eff[J] ~ -1/2 ∫ J G(Box) J`.

Therefore the same physical two-point kernel can be produced by an ordinary continuum/tower of local mediator modes carrying the same spectral density.

The representation may be viewed as a continuous generalization of the finite auxiliary-field localization of rational kernels.

## 3. Exact attribution consequence

Within the scope above,

`positive spectral branch cut / unusual propagator`

is observationally equivalent at the two-point level to

`continuum of ordinary local modes coupled to J`.

Hence it cannot by itself establish intrinsic quantum-gravity origin.

KMQGB classification:

`TWO_POINT_KL_CONTINUUM__E2_C4_C6_COMPARATOR_REQUIRED`.

This applies even when `rho` is highly constrained or uniquely generated by a finite rule.

Predictivity of the spectral density is scientifically valuable but distinct from gravity attribution.

## 5. What can still be genuinely new

The gate is **not** a no-go for nonlocal quantum gravity. It says the novelty cannot reside only in the positive two-point spectral function.

A serious O3 parent must provide at least one of the following beyond the Gaussian continuum representation:

### K1 — non-Gaussian higher-point rigidity

The same finite parent data must fix 3-point/4-point/higher spectral vertices or cumulants in a way not reproducible by an arbitrary continuum mediator model.

### K2 — cross-representation rigidity

The same object must fix both the hard scattering relation and retarded/CTP response, with shared parameters and phases.

### K3 — intrinsically gravitational constraint/Ward relation

A higher-point identity must depend on gravitational gauge/constraint structure and survive profiling over ordinary matter/source continua.

### K4 — controlled violation of standard KL assumptions

If the parent has no standard positive KL representation because of entire nonlocality, modified quantum dynamics, nonstandard causality or another escape, the corresponding unitarity/causality/positivity taxes must be paid explicitly.
