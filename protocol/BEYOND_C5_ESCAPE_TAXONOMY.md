# Beyond-C5 Escape Taxonomy for Future Candidate Gravity

**Status:** frozen design methodology / no Candidate Gravity ansatz promoted.  
**Purpose:** force every future `DeltaGamma_KG` seed to state exactly which structural assumption lets it escape the full matched low-energy gravitational EFT/C5 parent.

## 1. Baseline containment statement

Within the declared low-energy domain, a deformation that is simultaneously

- local;
- analytic in momenta/derivatives order by order;
- diffeomorphism invariant;
- metric-only with the ordinary massless spin-2 sector;
- governed by standard unitary quantum field theory;
- and organized as the usual derivative expansion,

belongs to the gravitational EFT theory space represented by full matched C5.

Therefore a future KG-specific `DeltaGamma` cannot be justified by merely adding another local higher-curvature Wilson coefficient.

This is a benchmark design statement, not a mathematical claim that every UV completion is C5.

## 2. Five structural escape doors

A proposed beyond-C5 seed must identify at least one of the following doors.

### E1 — nonlocal / nonanalytic dynamics

Examples: fixed nonlocal form factors, nonlocal vertices, branch structures not reducible to the matched C5 loop/EFT sector.

Mandatory taxes:

- distinguish from ordinary massless-loop nonanalyticity;
- field-redefinition/on-shell-equivalence audit;
- form-factor rigidity: no arbitrary functional fitting;
- retarded support, no-signalling, micro/global causality and initial-value audit;
- common-domain UV/nonlocal comparator.

A zero-free entire propagator by itself is not enough; known EOM-squared classes can be tree-amplitude equivalent to the underlying local theory.

### E2 — additional propagating degrees of freedom / mediator

Examples: scalar, vector, massive spin-2, continuum/tower or hidden quantum mediator.

Mandatory taxes:

- C4 and modified-gravity/scalar-tensor/bimetric/UV-completion quotient;
- ghost/tachyon/positivity audit;
- prove the new degree is gravitational rather than an ordinary quantum force;
- universal stress-energy / helicity/tidal attribution across source sectors.

A quantum mediator plus a Newtonian-looking force is not gravity attribution.

### E3 — genuinely nonperturbative / transseries sector

Examples: effects nonanalytic in the perturbative coupling or invisible to every finite local derivative order, such as instanton/saddle/transseries sectors.

Mandatory taxes:

- explicit parent derivation of the nonperturbative weight and phase;
- show it is not merely a state/topology choice already permitted by the comparator parent;
- define a Lorentzian/observable continuation rather than only an Euclidean saddle;
- identify the regime where it is measurable yet the parent approximation is controlled;
- compare against other nonperturbative gravity/UV-completion sectors.

An exponential suppression factor is not novelty without a fixed parent saddle and observable map.

### E4 — modified quantum dynamics / open-system fundamental law

Examples: fundamental Lindblad/CTP/hybrid modifications beyond standard unitary closed-system QFT.

Mandatory taxes:

- distinguish from ordinary reduced dynamics obtained by integrating out an environment;
- complete positivity/unitarity/reality and energy-momentum consistency;
- no-signalling/locality;
- C2/C3/C3b/classical-CQ and generic-bath quotient;
- gravity/mediator attribution.

A generic noise/dissipation kernel is not a new law.

### E5 — modified symmetry / causal / kinematic structure

Examples: modified Lorentz/diffeomorphism realization, discrete causal structure, deformed locality or process structure.

Mandatory taxes:

- recover observed Lorentz/diffeomorphism/GR limits or state the controlled breaking;
- constraint algebra/physical-DOF closure;
- QRF/relational formulation;
- distinguish geometry attribution from ordinary quantum control;
- phenomenological bounds on symmetry breaking;
- causal/no-signalling consistency.

A bare superposition of coordinates/events or indefinite causal order is not sufficient.

## 3. Combined-door candidates

A serious seed may use more than one door, but doors do not cancel each other's obligations.

The currently least-trivial same-spin-2 direction is conceptually an **E1/E3-like parent-fixed deformation**:

- preserve the GR massless pole and low-energy Ward structure;
- avoid arbitrary local Wilson-coefficient freedom;
- introduce a finite-parameter nonlocal/nonperturbative relation;
- link several response orders/configurations from one parent;
- survive field-redefinition, state, extra-mediator and full-C5 profiling;
- pass explicit causality and global comparator tests.

This statement identifies a search region, **not a Candidate Gravity ansatz**.

## 4. Escape-door declaration required in machine records

Every future exploratory candidate record should add

- `escape_doors`: one or more of `E1`–`E5`;
- `escape_claim`: exact reason full matched C5 does not contain the seed;
- `escape_evidence_refs`;
- `door_specific_blockers`.

If `escape_claim` is absent or reduces to a feature already contained by a comparator, G5 remains `BLOCKED` or `FAIL` and promotion is forbidden.

## 5. Cross-order rigidity requirement

Escaping C5 at one isolated observable is not sufficient.

A preferred seed should generate from the same shared parameters at least two linked sectors, for example

- two-point spectral/retarded structure plus a higher-point Ward-complete vertex;
- low-order source response plus a held-out higher-order response;
- static/soft gravity attribution plus a nonlocal cross-regime observable.

The candidate is stronger when the shared-parent stacking creates positive

`R_shared = sum rank(J_block) - rank(J_stack)`

and a nonzero global comparator-orthogonal residual survives.

## 6. Current forbidden shortcut

Do not call an exploratory seed Candidate Gravity merely because it uses one escape door.  The complete Promotion Gate remains mandatory.
