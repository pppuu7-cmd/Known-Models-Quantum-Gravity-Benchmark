# Radiative Protection and Full-C5 Three-Point Gate

**Status:** frozen pre-ansatz methodology / no Candidate Gravity promotion.  
**Purpose:** correct the quartic-first search so that “preserve the GR three-point vertex” is interpreted consistently with loop-generated structures already present in ordinary quantum GR/C5.

## 1. The problem with literal `C3=0`

Pure four-dimensional Einstein gravity is not perturbatively closed under loops. At two loops the on-shell UV divergence contains a nonzero cubic-curvature counterterm of Goroff-Sagnotti type,

`Gamma_div^(2) proportional to integral sqrt(-g) Riemann^3`.

In a common convention the coefficient is

`209 / [2880 (4 pi)^4 epsilon]`

multiplying the cubic Riemann contraction, up to the normalization conventions for the gravitational coupling/action.

Therefore an exploratory statement

`total cubic curvature coefficient = 0 at all quantum orders`

is not generically stable for an ordinary quantized metric theory unless a parent symmetry/completion enforces the cancellation/protection.

## 2. Correct quartic-first statement

Replace the naive rule

`GR three-point exactly forever`

with the scoped rule

> **no new independent cubic graviton deformation beyond the full matched C5 prediction in the same order/domain.**

Thus the comparator contains

- Einstein tree-level three-point structure;
- loop-generated nonanalytic pieces;
- required local counterterms/Wilson coefficients such as the two-loop cubic-curvature direction;
- state/renormalization-scheme dependence where physically applicable;
- exact Ward/contact completion.

Candidate novelty begins only after this full matched three-point/C5 sector is subtracted/profiled.

## 3. Radiative-protection requirement

If a future KG parent claims that a cubic structure is absent or fixed beyond the ordinary C5 value, it must supply one of:

1. an exact symmetry/selection rule forbidding the operator;
2. a UV completion that fixes/cancels the coefficient and its running;
3. a nonperturbative identity relating the cubic sector to higher vertices;
4. an explicit renormalization-group invariant relation showing stability under the declared quantum dynamics.

A tree-level zero without protection is not a rigid prediction.

## 4. Type-II string as protection precedent

Type-II string theory provides a useful structural precedent:

- its tree-level alpha-prime expansion has no lower `R^2`/`R^3` correction in the pure graviton sector;
- the first alpha-prime correction is `alpha'^3 R^4`;
- the absence and tensor structure follow from the parent string/supersymmetry/worldsheet organization rather than choosing Wilson coefficients independently.

This does not mean a future KG must be supersymmetric or stringy. It establishes the standard of evidence required for a protected quartic-first hierarchy.

## 5. Loop/order matching in the machine pipeline

Every candidate record using a quartic-first claim should distinguish

- `tree_newphysics_C3`;
- `C5_loop_C3`;
- `candidate_loop_or_parent_C3`;
- renormalized physical three-point/amplitude object in the common domain.

The comparator quotient is performed on the **renormalized matched physical object**, not on a bare tree coefficient.

## 6. RG mixing / operator closure

A future parent action must audit whether the declared quartic/higher operator set is closed under renormalization in the scoped approximation.

If loops generate lower/higher operators not included in the candidate ledger, response completeness is `BLOCKED` until those directions are incorporated or proven absent by symmetry.

This is the radiative analogue of ordered-partition response completeness.

## 7. Causality interaction

The CEMZ three-point causality tax applies to additional higher-derivative three-graviton structures in the relevant weakly coupled high-energy regime.

The radiative gate therefore asks two separate questions:

1. what cubic structure is unavoidably present after full C5/renormalization matching?
2. does the candidate introduce an additional cubic deformation whose high-energy causal completion must be explained?

Do not confuse a loop-generated C5 counterterm with a claimed new tree-level KG deformation.

## 8. Updated quartic-first architecture

The preferred search language becomes

`full matched C5 through 3-point + first candidate-specific independent deformation at 4-point/higher`.

This is stronger and more accurate than saying simply `C3=0`.

## 9. Promotion guardrail

A quartic-first KG record may not pass its parent/rigidity gate if

- lower-order operators are set to zero without radiative protection;
- loop-generated full-C5 structures are omitted from the comparator;
- RG mixing is ignored in the declared domain;
- the claimed first-new-order changes after including mandatory quantum corrections.

No Candidate Gravity ansatz is promoted by this protocol alone.
