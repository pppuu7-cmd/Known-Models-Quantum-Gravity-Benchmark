**Status:** comparator-frontier upgrade.

## 1. Comparator architecture

Causal fermion systems (CFS) formulate spacetime through a measure on a set of finite-rank/self-adjoint operator data on a Hilbert space. The physical equations are imposed by minimizing a nonlinear causal action under constraints.

The architecture is therefore schematically

`Hilbert/operator data + universal measure + causal spectral Lagrangian + constrained minimization -> physical spacetime/dynamics`.

A 2026 Classical and Quantum Gravity paper strengthens the modern interpretation: spacetime is described as a web of correlations of a many-body quantum system; in a relevant subset that includes the continuum Minkowski vacuum limit, minimization of the causal action can be understood as minimization of fluctuations in causal structure.

This is directly relevant to the KMQGB Iter132 search because it is a non-RG, nonlinear, operator-valued origin architecture rather than a chosen propagator or ordinary functional-flow ansatz.

## 2. Why it is a strong comparator

CFS is not merely kinematic. Existing programme results connect the causal action principle to

- an emergent continuum spacetime description;
- Einstein-type classical gravity in an appropriate continuum limit;
- quantum-field/Fock-space limiting structures under additional assumptions;
- nonlinear many-body/operator correlations as fundamental data.

Therefore a future proposal of the form

> choose quantum gravity by minimizing a causal fluctuation/action functional over relational operator-valued spacetime data

is not architecture-new by itself.

## 3. A2 / origin-data lesson

The existence of a variational principle does not automatically make the physical solution unique or low-freedom in the KMQGB sense.

A complete parent must still specify/derive, as applicable,

- the Hilbert/operator domain and spin dimension;
- the admissible measure class and constraints;
- the causal Lagrangian/action parameters;
- regularization/continuum-limit data;
- physical state/boundary conditions;
- the rule selecting a physical minimizer when the variational problem has multiple admissible solutions;
- the asymptotic observable/LSZ or detector map.

Thus `finite-looking action principle != automatically unique hard dynamics`.

## 4. A3 rule

For any future operator-measure/causal-variance parent ask immediately:

`CFS1` Is spacetime represented through operator-valued measure/correlation data?

`CFS2` Is the dynamics selected by minimizing a nonlinear spectral/causal action?

`CFS3` Is the claimed GR limit structurally the same kind of continuum/emergent limit already studied in CFS?

`CFS4` Are different regularization/measure/minimizer choices being mistaken for physical novelty?

`CFS5` Does the candidate produce a prospectively normalized comparator-orthogonal hard `4g`/higher relation and same-parent CTP observable not already reproducible within the CFS architecture?

Without CFS5, classify the construction as comparator-contained or unresolved rather than novel.

## 5. Current CFS limitation does not create automatic novelty

The current CFS research programme itself identifies important open problems, including a more direct connection to gravity without relying only on the continuum limit and a fuller derivation of quantum/Fock dynamics and corrections.

Those open problems are not a license to rename the same architecture as a new KG parent. A candidate must derive a distinct hard relation rather than merely fill an implementation gap inside the comparator.
