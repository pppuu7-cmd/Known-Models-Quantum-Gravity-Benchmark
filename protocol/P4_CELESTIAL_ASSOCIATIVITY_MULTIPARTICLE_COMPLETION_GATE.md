## 2. Single-particle truncation is not the full celestial Hilbert/operator space

Bulk scattering contains multi-particle factorization and non-factorization channels. The celestial dual therefore contains multi-particle operators, not only single-particle primaries/currents.

Recent explicit constructions derive multi-particle celestial OPEs from

- bulk multi-collinear limits;

## 3. Associativity/Jacobi constraints are truncation-sensitive

A single-particle celestial algebra may obey a simple Jacobi/double-residue condition only when multi-particle branch/cut contributions are absent or consistently projected away.

Multi-collinear analyses show explicitly that multi-particle channels can create extra OPE terms and branch structures. Consequently, failure of the truncated single-particle Jacobi condition need not mean the full bulk theory is inconsistent; the missing contribution may reside in the multi-particle operator sector.

Conversely, unusually strong algebraic rigidity in a single-particle/chiral/self-dual truncation is not automatically rigidity of the complete hard S-matrix.
