## 1. Process-matrix setting

In finite-dimensional operational formulations, a quantum process can be represented by a positive semidefinite process/comb operator `W` subject to linear normalization/causality constraints ensuring valid probabilities for allowed local quantum operations.

Abstractly, the admissible set has the form

`C = { W >= 0 | L_a(W)=c_a }`.

This is an intersection of the positive cone with an affine linear subspace and is therefore convex.

The constraints define **which processes are allowed**. They do not generally select one distinguished `W`.

## 2. A2 — consistency leaves process freedom

Unless the physical constraints reduce `C` to a single normalized ray/point, there are distinct admissible processes

`W1 != W2`

and every convex combination

`lambda W1 + (1-lambda) W2`, `0<=lambda<=1`,

is also admissible.

Thus

`positivity + normalization + causal/process consistency != unique dynamics`.

A P4 candidate must derive the actual process operator/kernel from additional finite physical data rather than selecting one post hoc.

Classification:

`A2 BLOCKED__PROCESS_CONSISTENCY_DEFINES_CONVEX_FEASIBLE_SET_NOT_UNIQUE_GRAVITY_PROCESS`.

## 3. Gravity-attribution problem

Indefinite causal order is not by itself a quantum-gravity certificate.

A 2025 relativistic analysis of operational causal order shows that the relevant causal-order observable does not distinguish an optical quantum switch from a gedanken gravitational quantum switch based on a superposition of gravitational fields. The operational phenomenon can therefore occur without the hard dynamics being quantum gravity.

Hence

`indefinite causal order != gravity attribution`.

Classification:

`A3 ATTRIBUTION_BLOCKED__INDEFINITE_CAUSAL_ORDER_HAS_NONGRAVITATIONAL_REALIZATIONS`.

## 4. Relation to Stinespring/O4 squeeze

Earlier KMQGB O4 work established

- reduced linear CPTP dynamics can be represented through ordinary unitary system+environment dilation;
- generic deterministic nonlinear state dynamics faces no-signalling/EPR problems.

A process-matrix formulation can enlarge the operational causal structure beyond a simple reduced channel, which is a legitimate escape from the narrow Stinespring statement.

But the present gate shows the new problem:

`larger admissible process space != unique gravity law`.

The full process must still be selected and attributed physically.
