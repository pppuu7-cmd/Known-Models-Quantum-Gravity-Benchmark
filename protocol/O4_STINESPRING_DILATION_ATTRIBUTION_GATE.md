# O4 Stinespring-Dilation Attribution Gate

**Status:** permanent E4/O4 attribution gate.  
**KMQGB iteration:** 080.  
**Purpose:** prevent a completely-positive reduced quantum channel or Lindblad-like gravitational decoherence law from being labeled a fundamental modification of quantum mechanics when the same observed channel can be produced by ordinary unitary system-environment dynamics.

## 1. Scope

Let the experimentally accessible quantum sector evolve over a finite interval by a completely positive trace-preserving map

`rho -> E(rho)`.

The statement below concerns the observable quantum channel. A full classical-quantum hybrid theory may contain additional classical registers/fields and must be audited separately.

## 2. Stinespring representation

For any CPTP map `E`, Stinespring dilation gives an auxiliary Hilbert space `H_env`, an environment state `|0>` (or density matrix), and an isometry/unitary extension such that

`E(rho) = Tr_env[ U (rho ⊗ |0><0|) U^dagger ]`

up to the standard extension of an isometry to a unitary on a sufficiently large space.

Therefore measurements restricted to the system channel cannot, by themselves, distinguish

`fundamental nonunitary/CPTP law`

from

`ordinary global unitary law + unobserved environment`.

Continuous-time Markovian Lindblad semigroups have corresponding unitary dilation constructions with suitable environments/quantum noise.

## 3. KMQGB attribution consequence

A gravity-linked CPTP/Lindblad/decoherence signature is not a fundamental E4 novelty certificate solely because

- it destroys coherence;
- it produces diffusion/noise;
- it has a gravity-dependent rate;
- it is written as a stochastic or Lindblad equation;
- no explicit environment was included in the phenomenological model.

At the observable channel level an ordinary environment can reproduce the same map.

Classification:

`CPTP_CHANNEL_ONLY__DILATABLE_ENVIRONMENT_COMPARATOR_REQUIRED`.

## 4. What an O4 survivor must add

A serious fundamental modified-quantum-dynamics proposal must provide a relation that survives the dilation loophole. At least one of the following is required.

### D1 — global cross-sector inconsistency with all allowed dilations

Show that reproducing the proposed channel with an environment would violate independently frozen energy-momentum, locality, covariance, state-counting or gravitational constraints.

### D2 — gravity-specific joint observable

Predict a joint matter+gravity observable, not merely the reduced matter channel, whose correlations cannot be generated by the registered ordinary quantum environment/mediator comparator under the same constraints.

### D3 — hard spin-2 CTP rigidity

Derive a hard graviton/CTP vertex relation from the same finite modified-dynamics law. Generic bath noise kernels do not count; the tensor/helicity and cross-order structure must be fixed.

### D4 — controlled departure from CPTP quantum-channel assumptions

If the fundamental law is not CPTP in the usual sense, explicitly establish probability consistency/no-signalling/positivity and state what replaces the standard quantum operational framework. This is a much higher consistency tax, not an automatic escape.

## 5. Relation to classical-quantum hybrid gravity

A hybrid theory with a classical metric register and quantum matter can be more structured than a reduced quantum channel. The correct comparator is then an enlarged classical-quantum stochastic/unitary model, not Stinespring alone.

The gate therefore does **not** claim all hybrid gravity theories are mathematically equivalent to ordinary unitary quantum gravity.

It makes the narrower statement:

> reduced CPTP decoherence/diffusion data alone cannot certify a fundamental modification of quantum theory.

The full classical metric statistics, backreaction law and joint correlations must carry the novelty burden.

## 6. Hard-scope consequence

For KMQGB P4, an O4 proposal is not worth promotion based only on laboratory decoherence/noise rates. It must link the same finite E4 law to

`hard physical spin2 response/scattering + CTP/retarded structure + joint gravity/matter observable`

and survive ordinary environment/source/mediator profiling.

## 7. Score consequence

This is a necessary attribution gate, not a P4 survivor. R4 remains unchanged.
