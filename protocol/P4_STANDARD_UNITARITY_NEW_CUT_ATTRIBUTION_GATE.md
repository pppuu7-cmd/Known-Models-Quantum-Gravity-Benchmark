# P4 Standard-Unitarity New-Cut Attribution Gate

**Status:** permanent hard-singularity P4 prefilter.  
**KMQGB iteration:** 095.  
**Purpose:** prevent an unexplained four-point branch cut from being treated as a new irreducible quantum-gravity datum when ordinary S-matrix unitarity already ties physical discontinuities to intermediate states and lower transition amplitudes.

## 1. Unitarity identity

For `S=1+iT`, ordinary unitarity gives

`-i(T-T^dagger)=T^dagger T`.

Between external states this becomes a sum/integral over a complete set of physical intermediate states `X`:

`Disc / Im A(I->F) ~ sum_X integral dPi_X A(I->X) A(F->X)^*`,

with the exact normalization depending on conventions.

This is the nonperturbative optical-theorem content. Perturbative Cutkosky rules are the corresponding diagrammatic realization for discontinuities of Feynman amplitudes.

## 2. Consequence for a proposed new hard cut

Suppose a candidate and comparator have, in a declared common domain,

- the same physical Hilbert/intermediate-state spectrum relevant to the cut;
- the same lower transition amplitudes entering the unitarity sum;
- the same standard composition/unitarity law.

Then the physical discontinuity constructed from those data is the same.

Therefore a genuinely new physical four-point cut requires at least one of

1. new/modified intermediate states or continuum support;
2. changed lower amplitudes/couplings feeding the cut;
3. a nonperturbative sector not present in the comparator's complete transition data;
4. modified factorization/probability/unitarity law.

A branch function cannot be inserted independently and then called intrinsic gravity.

## 3. Comparator routing

### New intermediate support

Route to E2/C4/C6/string/tower/continuum-mediator comparators.

### Same particles but changed lower amplitudes

The first novelty is actually lower-point data and must be attributed there; the cut is downstream evidence.

### Standard GR/matter multiparticle support

Route to matched GR loops/full C5/ordinary matter quantum sectors.

### Modified unitarity/factorization

Route to E4/E5 and apply positivity, probability, composition, causality and no-signalling taxes.

## 4. Relation to anomalous thresholds

Landau/anomalous thresholds do not evade the attribution logic. Physical-region Landau singularities correspond to allowed on-shell internal processes, and their discontinuities obey cutting rules. They may have nontrivial kinematic locations, but their origin is still tied to the spectrum and couplings of the underlying theory.

## 5. Same-spectrum synthesis consequence

For a future parent that deliberately preserves the ordinary GR asymptotic spectrum and complete lower transition data, an unexplained new positive-unitarity branch cut is not an admissible first hard datum.

The remaining same-spectrum escape must instead be something such as

- a pole/cut-free nonlocal structure with independently derived causal origin;
- an intrinsic nonperturbative/transseries contribution whose physical contour/weight is fixed by the parent;
- or a controlled modification of the quantum/factorization law.
