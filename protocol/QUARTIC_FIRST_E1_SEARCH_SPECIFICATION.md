`S_4skel = S_EH + (lambda4/M^4) int sqrt(-g) V4[C,nabla;Psi_M]`.

`V4 ~ (C_mnrs C^mnrs) Psi_M(Box) (C_abcd C^abcd)`.

## 2. Exact lower-order anchors

For a regular quartic-curvature parent around Minkowski,

`V4 = O(h^4)`.

Therefore

- the quadratic/free propagator is exactly Einstein-Hilbert;
- the massless spin-2 pole/residue are exactly the GR ones;
- the cubic graviton vertex is exactly Einstein-Hilbert;
- the specific higher-derivative three-point causality gate associated with modifying the graviton cubic coupling is avoided by construction.

These are anchors, not novelty claims.

## 3. Low-energy containment remains C5

If `Psi_M` is analytic at `Box=0`,

`Psi_M(Box) = psi_0 + psi_1 Box/M^2 + ...`.

At any finite derivative order the resulting `C^4`, `D^2 C^4`, ... structures belong to the local gravitational EFT/on-shell operator basis and must be profiled as C5.

Hence the candidate may seek distinction only in a **parent-fixed all-orders cross-regime relation**, not in an individual low-energy Wilson coefficient.

## 4. First physical novelty target

The first modified pure-gravity on-shell block is the real four-graviton amplitude.

A complete block must include

- all `O(h^4)` contact structures generated by the exact parent `V4`;
- all tensor/helicity structures fixed by the parent;

Because the GR cubic vertex is unchanged, there is no new cubic-exchange contribution at linear order in `lambda4` unless the exact parent introduces additional nonlocal/auxiliary structures.

## 5. Four-graviton causality/dispersion tax

Avoiding a modified three-point vertex does not remove UV consistency requirements.

In weakly coupled four-dimensional gravity, causality and unitarity yield dispersion relations that place two-sided constraints on higher-derivative four-graviton Wilson coefficients and relate their allowed size to UV/higher-spin data.

Therefore the exact parent must establish

1. crossing symmetry;
2. perturbative/full unitarity in its declared domain;
3. treatment of the massless graviton pole in dispersion relations;
4. bounded/controlled Regge or high-energy behavior;
5. absence of unacceptable time advances/causal pathologies;
6. the interpretation of any UV spectral/high-spin scale, or an explicit demonstration that an assumption of the weakly-coupled dispersion framework is replaced.

No claim of “same spin-2 only at all scales” is authorized merely because the low-order action has no extra pole.

## 6. Spectral positivity is only a filter

A positive spectral density or unchanged free spectrum does not select `Psi_M` uniquely.

Entire/nonlocal form-factor families can satisfy such spectral consistency while retaining broad functional freedom.  Kernel selection therefore requires a stronger parent principle that also fixes higher vertices and the Lorentzian/CTP prescription.

A viable parent must predict a **fixed vector of helicity/tensor coefficients** rather than one independently fitted coefficient per channel.

This provides a natural rigidity observable:

- fit/anchor at one subset of helicity/kinematic points;
- predict the remaining crossing/helicity blocks with the same `(lambda4,M)` and parent-fixed tensor ratios;
- profile the full local C5 basis in the IR;
- test the resummed cross-regime shape against same-domain UV/nonlocal comparators.
