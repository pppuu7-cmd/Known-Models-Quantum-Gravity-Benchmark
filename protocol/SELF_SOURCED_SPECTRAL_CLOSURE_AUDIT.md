# Self-Sourced Spectral Closure (SSC) — Constructive P4 Audit

**Status:** frozen exploratory audit / no KG promotion.  
**Purpose:** test whether a no-new-species self-consistency principle can generate a hard quantum-gravity spectral/amplitude structure without an arbitrary form factor.

## 1. Proposed SSC principle

Exploratory principle:

> The hard spectral weight is generated entirely by the graviton/multigraviton states of the same parent dynamics. The spectral function used inside the quantum equations is the same full spectral function solved for externally. No independent phenomenological form factor or manually inserted CDD/new-particle pole is allowed.

A strong SSC implementation would require

- crossing/unitarity;
- self-consistent multigraviton spectral support;
- GR infrared normalization;
- a Lorentzian/retarded prescription;
- a unique or low-dimensional subtraction/renormalization rule;
- higher-point/CTP closure.

## 2. Strong SSC comparator — spectral RG / asymptotic safety

Modern Lorentzian spectral-renormalisation-group gravity already performs a very close realization of the proposed self-consistency condition: the full nonperturbative graviton spectral function, including the scattering continuum, is used self-consistently inside the diagrams.

The resulting spectral function has

- a positive massless one-graviton peak;
- a positive multigraviton continuum;
- physical on-shell normalization and unit total spectral weight in the reported scheme;
- nonperturbative UV scaling.

KMQGB classification:

`STRONG_SSC_REALIZATION__ASYMPTOTIC_SAFETY_COMPARATOR`.

Thus spectral self-consistency by itself is not a new KG parent principle.

## 3. EFT unitarization / graviball branch

Nonperturbative unitarization of perturbative graviton-graviton amplitudes can generate a scalar graviton-graviton resonance (“graviball”) without inserting an elementary scalar state.

This is a valuable proof that collective graviton dynamics can create nonperturbative spectral structure.

However the audited constructions use specific unitarization procedures (inverse-amplitude / N-D-type methods), contain scheme/separation-scale choices, and do not by themselves define a unique full crossing-complete all-channel quantum-gravity parent with all inelastic multiparticle sectors.

KMQGB classification:

`COLLECTIVE_SPECTRAL_ORIGIN_PRECEDENT__INSUFFICIENT_UNIQUE_PARENT_CLOSURE`.

## 4. No-new-CDD/no-new-species condition is insufficient

Forbidding additional elementary poles does not uniquely determine

- the continuum spectral density;
- inelastic channel weights;
- subtraction constants;
- partial-wave completion;
- crossing completion;
- higher-point/CTP vertices.

Therefore `no new species` is a restriction, not a kernel-selection principle.

## 5. Candidate Gravity consequence

A future SSC-like KG principle must differ from known AS/spectral-RG constructions by a concrete common-domain relation, not merely by adopting self-consistency language.

It would need to derive, with fewer assumptions/parameters,

`rho_hard + 4pt amplitude + higher-point/CTP hierarchy`

and survive direct profiling against the asymptotic-safety spectral function/scattering manifold and ordinary unitarized-GR collective resonances.

## 6. Terminal verdict

`NO_CONSTRUCTIVE_P4_SURVIVOR`.

SSC is a strong design idea but is already substantially realized by an existing asymptotic-safety comparator in its strong spectral form, while weaker unitarization forms do not uniquely close the parent dynamics.

R4 remains unchanged. No KG ansatz is promoted.
