# P4 Boundary-at-Infinity Origin-Data Gate

**Status:** scoped tree-level hard-amplitude prefilter.  
**KMQGB iteration:** 108.  
**Purpose:** identify where genuinely new rational tree-level hard data can hide once ordinary finite factorization poles are frozen.

## 1. Gravity constructibility control

For the standard two-line BCFW deformation, tree-level graviton amplitudes have the improved large-`z` behavior that removes the residue at infinity. The physical amplitude is then reconstructed from finite factorization poles and lower-point amplitudes.

If the three-point seed and finite pole residues are exactly the Einstein ones, this reconstructible branch returns ordinary Einstein tree gravity.

Recent 2025–2026 work further shows that other multi-line/hard shifts can have nonzero residues at infinity and that those boundary terms may display nontrivial factorization-like/soft-related structure; however general consistency principles do not generically fix all such residues.

## 2. Where a new rational same-spectrum datum could live

For a rational tree amplitude with the same complete finite poles and residues as the comparator,

`Delta M_n = M_n(new) - M_n(comp)`

has no finite factorization poles.

A pole-free rational function of the complex deformation variable is polynomial in that variable. In local QFT language this is a boundary/contact contribution.

Therefore, under the stated rational/local assumptions,

`new same-spectrum rational hard datum -> boundary-at-infinity/contact datum`.

If that contact is local analytic, it lies in full C5 after matching.

## 3. Escape routes

A candidate escapes this gate only by explicitly supplying at least one of

1. new finite poles/states/residues (E2/tower comparator route);
2. non-rational/nonanalytic hard structure (cuts, essential/exponential growth, infinite poles/zeros);
3. an intrinsic nonperturbative sector not captured by the rational tree description;
4. modified factorization/quantum law.

A nonzero boundary term is not by itself novelty: higher-derivative local interactions routinely create boundary contributions.

## 4. Boundary factorization is transport, not origin

If a boundary-at-infinity term can itself be written recursively in terms of lower Einstein amplitudes or mapped to ordinary soft factors, this is a useful compression/consistency relation but remains GR-contained.

A P4 claim needs a **physical law selecting the boundary datum itself** and must explain why the selected law differs from

- ordinary GR large-`z`/soft behavior;
- local higher-derivative contact terms;
- string/dual-resonant infinite-pole behavior;
- known weakly-nonlocal/exponential form-factor behavior.
