# P4 Eikonal Causality Selector-Limit Gate

**Status:** permanent causality/attribution prefilter.  
**KMQGB iteration:** 110.  
**Purpose:** prevent absence of eikonal time advance from being treated as a unique origin law for a quantum-gravity hard interaction.

## 1. Why the criterion is important

High-energy small-angle gravitational scattering can encode a Shapiro/eikonal time delay. Higher-derivative corrections to graviton interactions can alter the phase shift and, in suitable regimes/polarizations, produce a time advance.

Classic causality analysis of modified graviton three-point couplings shows that some problematic corrections cannot be repaired by adding only finitely many conventional spin `J<=2` states and instead point toward a tower of higher-spin states.

This is an important UV/completeness constraint.

## 2. But causality is a regime-dependent inequality, not generally a unique selector

A candidate principle of the form

> choose the gravitational hard interaction as the unique theory with no resolvable time advance

is too strong.

Recent work stresses that flat-space/eikonal causality constraints must remain inside the weak-gravity and EFT-validity regime. Apparent advances outside that regime do not constitute a controlled exclusion.

Further, asymptotic causal structure is dimension-sensitive. A 2026 analysis finds that in four spacetime dimensions prompt asymptotic null curves in the class studied can be insensitive to higher-derivative corrections, whereas in higher dimensions genuine asymptotic superluminality can constrain the EFT regime.

Therefore the condition is not a universal one-equation selector of four-dimensional hard gravity.

## 3. Allowed-region rather than unique-point structure

At fixed derivative order, time-delay/positivity requirements typically impose inequalities or sign constraints on Wilson combinations.

Schematically,

`Delta t(b,E,polarization; c_i) >= 0`

cuts out an allowed region in coefficient space. Unless an independent principle forces simultaneous saturation of enough independent inequalities and proves that this saturation is physically mandatory, the admissible region remains finite- or higher-dimensional.

Thus

`causal allowed region != unique parent`.

## 4. Tower consequence is comparator pressure, not novelty

When a hard correction triggers the CEMZ-type obstruction, a higher-spin tower may be required before the problematic scale.

For KMQGB this means

`causality violation repaired by tower`

routes immediately to

- string/dual-resonant towers;
- higher-spin completion;
- KK/extended-state sectors where applicable;
- other E2 completeness architectures.

The tower must be derived from the candidate parent; inserting one ad hoc does not rescue P4.

## 5. Extremality caveat

One might try to strengthen the rule to

`choose the theory saturating the causal/time-delay bound`.

This still requires an independently motivated choice of

- background/scattering geometry;
- impact-parameter and energy domain;
- polarization functional;
- which bound is to be saturated;
- why saturation rather than strict inequality is fundamental.

Without such a law, extremality merely moves the missing physics into the objective/boundary condition, analogous to the earlier bootstrap-extremality failure.

## 6. P4 use

Eikonal causality should be used as

1. a post-A1/A2 consistency gate;
2. a trigger for E2/tower completion tests;
3. a constraint on admissible nonlocality/growth;
4. a cross-check that the candidate is tested inside its validity regime.

It should **not** be counted as selection of the first hard datum unless a separate gravity-native law proves a unique causal solution.

Classification for a causality-only parent:

`CAUSALITY_CONSISTENCY_ONLY__HARD_DATUM_NOT_UNIQUELY_SELECTED`.
