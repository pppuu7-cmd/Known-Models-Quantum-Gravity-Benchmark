# P4 Constructive Search — Negative Ledger

**Status:** frozen search ledger / does not increase R4.  
**Purpose:** preserve constructive-search branches that were examined and rejected as P4 parent selectors, so future chats do not repeatedly audit the same ideas.

## P4 standard

A P4 survivor must prospectively provide all of:

1. a low-freedom physical parent principle rather than an arbitrary function fit;
2. an explicit hard 4-point/higher or cross-representation relation;
3. a declared spectral/analyticity branch (`S-local` or `D-nonlocal`);
4. scoped Ward/CTP/causality/radiative completion;
5. a path to exact source/native kinematic mapping;
6. immediate comparator survival against full C5, string/dual-resonance/bootstrap, asymptotic safety, spectral/noncommutative geometry and modern nonlocal alternatives.

Negative audits below do **not** raise R4 because the frozen P4 rubric requires a constructive survivor.

## N1 — causal-diamond / generalized-entropy dynamics

**Result:** `INSUFFICIENT_PARENT_SELECTION__CROSS_BACKGROUND_CONSTRAINT`.

Causal-diamond/closed-horizon thermodynamic and entanglement-equilibrium constructions can reproduce Einstein or general diffeomorphism-invariant higher-curvature equations once the relevant entropy/Lagrangian functional is supplied. They constrain consistency across geometry and thermodynamics, but do not uniquely select the quartic/higher Wilson hierarchy required for KG.

Use as a cross-background validation block, not as a kernel selector.

## N2 — operator-algebra emergence of spacetime

**Result:** `STRONG_EMERGENCE_CRITERION__NO_HARD_4PT_PARENT_YET`.

Recent operator-algebraic work reconstructs metric/curvature and the nonlinear Einstein equations from algebras of quantized matter fields in a semiclassical limit, and models finite-N/nonperturbative discreteness through algebraic/RMT completion.

Useful lesson: algebraic structure can encode geometry and nonperturbative spectral discreteness.

Current blocker for P4: no derived four-graviton hard amplitude/kernel or same-parent scattering/CTP hierarchy suitable for the KMQGB promotion pipeline. RMT completion is also not gravity-specific by itself.

## N3 — quantum equivalence principle

**Result:** `GRAVITY_ATTRIBUTION_PRINCIPLE__NOT_INTERACTION_HIERARCHY_SELECTOR`.

Quantum-equivalence-principle formulations provide universality relations for inertial/gravitational/rest mass operators and useful tests of species/state dependence.

They strengthen gravity attribution and cross-state universality, but do not determine a unique quantum-gravitational four-point/higher interaction kernel.

Use as an attribution/holdout constraint, not P4.

## N4 — black-hole self-completion / entropy-dominance spectral ideas

**Result:** `UV_SPECTRAL_MOTIVATION__NO_UNIQUE_SCATTERING_PARENT`.

Black-hole entropy, random-matrix spectral proposals and trans-Planckian black-hole dominance motivate nonperturbative high-energy spectral structure. They may supply candidate density-of-states scaling or universal chaotic statistics.

Current blocker: no unique derivation of the graviton 4-point/higher S-matrix and CTP kernel from these assumptions alone. Random-matrix universality is not gravity-specific, while detailed black-hole spectral proposals are model-dependent.

This branch can become P4 only if a controlled map

`black-hole spectral law -> graviton hard spectral density -> amplitude/CTP hierarchy`

is derived prospectively with few parameters.

## N5 — entanglement + retardation / graviton witness

**Result:** `OPERATIONAL_WITNESS__NOT_PARENT_DYNAMICS`.

Recent analyses indicate that observing retardation together with gravity-mediated entanglement would strengthen the case for propagating quantum gravitational degrees of freedom compared with a Newtonian instantaneous approximation.

This is highly relevant to the experimental attribution stack, but it does not select a fundamental quartic/higher parent dynamics and therefore is not P4.

Gluing a supplied physical projector does not uniquely select the projector or a new hard graviton kernel.

## Current constructive frontier

After these rejections, the strongest unresolved possibilities remain:

- `E3`: genuinely nonperturbative graviton dynamics that changes hard spectral weight without introducing an arbitrary Wilson/function tower;
- `E5+`: a microscopic dynamical object that derives both geometry **and** hard interaction data, rather than only a gluing/composition consistency scaffold;
- `D-nonlocal`: only if an additional physical principle uniquely or near-uniquely fixes the amplitude class and is not merely UV/IR reciprocity;
- `S-local + new spectral origin`: only if the new spectral measure/tower/continuum is itself derived by a low-freedom parent rule;
- `finite-object dynamical selector`: a finite microscopic rule that directly fixes a normalized 4-point/higher kernel and the same-parent CTP/retarded block with no independent per-order Wilson/function freedom.

R4 remains frozen until one branch produces an explicit survivor.
