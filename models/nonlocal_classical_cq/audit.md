## Terminal question

Does probe entanglement or a non-entanglement-breaking effective interaction prove a nonclassical gravitational mediator **without an explicit locality/microcausality assumption**?

**No.**

Recent hybrid classical-gravity analyses exhibit classical-gravity/quantum-matter models that can generate probe entanglement, with the mechanism traced to nonlocal structure. Therefore the local classical-channel no-entanglement logic cannot be extended to arbitrary nonlocal hybrid dynamics.

## Frozen literature result

Angeli & Carlesso (Phys. Rev. D 112, 024047 (2025)) analyze Markovian hybrid classical-quantum gravity models and prove that entanglement generation can occur. They identify the root of the entangling behavior in an underlying **nonlocality** of the theories, with the Diósi-Penrose model as a key example.

Trillo & Navascués (Phys. Rev. D 111, L121101 (2025)) likewise find gravitationally induced entanglement in the Diósi-Penrose model for certain parameter regimes.

Feng–Vedral–Marletto (Phys. Rev. D 113, 104055 (2026)) analyze this challenge and argue that the collapse-based examples do not violate the locality-based nonclassicality witness because the models contain nonlocal/hidden-detector features that violate the witness assumptions.

The literature therefore converges on the methodological point relevant to KMQGB even where interpretations differ:

`entanglement witness => mediator nonclassicality`

is conditional on a declared locality/information-flow assumption.

## Why this matters for Candidate Gravity

A KG model that beats C2/C3/C3b by predicting a non-EB channel is not automatically superior if it achieves this by inserting an acausal/nonlocal interaction kernel that bypasses the classical-channel theorem.

The candidate must distinguish:

1. **controlled nonlocality** compatible with its causal/analytic framework, if genuinely required by the theory;
2. **forbidden information nonlocality** that permits spacelike or unaccounted quantum transfer and makes the witness trivial.

Thus locality is part of the model consistency/attribution vector, not merely an experimental assumption.

## Relation to T4-05 and the GIE controversy

T4-05 showed that ordinary quantum matter can mimic non-EB/commutator features while gravity remains non-quantum unless mediator attribution is supplied.

T5-04 identifies a second independent loophole:

- even with no ordinary quantum mediator assigned to gravity, a sufficiently nonlocal hybrid dynamics can generate entanglement and evade the local classical-channel theorem.

The 2025–2026 debate about classical gravity and GIE is therefore best represented in KMQGB as two separate attribution checks:

`quantum-matter mediator attribution` and `locality attribution`.
