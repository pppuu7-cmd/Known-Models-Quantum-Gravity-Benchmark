# T7-02 Audit — squeezed / exotic graviton-state residual class

Benchmark ID: `KMQGB-T7-M02-SQUEEZED-STATE`

State: TERMINAL

## Claim under attack

Potential shortcut:

> enhanced noise, transition selection rules, stimulated graviton processes or entanglement caused by a squeezed graviton state demonstrate new gravity dynamics beyond C5.

## Comparator result

This claim is state-degenerate.

Squeezed, coherent and squeezed-coherent graviton states are ordinary non-vacuum states of a quantized gravitational field. Published calculations within standard perturbative graviton quantization show that squeezing can strongly enhance graviton noise/decoherence, alter detector transition probabilities and generate stimulated-emission or quantum-information signatures.

These effects can be striking compared with the **vacuum state**, but the correct comparator is not vacuum-only C5. It is

`C5 dynamics + admissible graviton state rho_h`.

Therefore a state-dependent signature does not identify a new gravitational parent dynamics unless the required state itself cannot arise within the allowed C5 state/preparation/history sector or unless a state-independent relation is violated.

## State-versus-dynamics quotient

For any proposed observable `O`, compare

`O_obs` versus `O_C5[rho_h, nuisance]`

and profile over physically admissible C5 graviton states consistent with the source/cosmological preparation assumptions.

A valid `DeltaGamma_KG` must not be removable merely by changing `rho_h`.

## Terminal classification

`OPERATIONALLY_DEGENERATE`

Claim: `SQUEEZED_OR_EXOTIC_GRAVITON_STATE_AS_NEW_DYNAMICS`.

## Candidate Gravity lesson

Separate **state novelty** from **dynamics novelty**. Squeezing may be an excellent amplifier/readout resource, but it is not a KG parent-dynamics residual by itself.
