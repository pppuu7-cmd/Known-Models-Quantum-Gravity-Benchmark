## Question

Can a future KG model infer causal consistency merely from a ghost-free/positive spectrum, UV finiteness or a healthy perturbative front velocity when the parent dynamics is nonlocal?

## Concrete literature boundary

Weakly/nonlocal entire-form-factor gravity can be constructed with no extra ghost-like poles and with good perturbative or macroscopic propagation properties in selected backgrounds.

However, Zhao, Modesto & Bambi (2026) explicitly show that a special but large subclass of nonlocal form factors admits Gödel-type **vacuum** solutions with closed timelike curves. Their result is compatible with quantum-level renormalizability/unitarity assumptions for the class and therefore demonstrates that those properties alone do not guarantee global causal consistency.

This is not a blanket proof that every ghost-free nonlocal theory is acausal. Different form factors/backgrounds can behave differently, and perturbative microcausal tails, front velocity, global chronology and the initial-value problem are distinct questions.
