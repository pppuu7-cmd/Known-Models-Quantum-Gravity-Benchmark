## Why raw sideband asymmetry is unsafe

For a generic measurement record, the measured spectrum is not simply the target force/displacement spectrum. Schematically it contains

`S_meas = S_target + S_imprecision + S_backaction + 2 Re S_cross`

with detector-dependent filtering/order conventions.

Børkje shows that the interpretation of optomechanical sideband asymmetry depends on detector ordering and can involve quantum optomechanical interference.

Novotny et al. construct a fully classical stochastic-electrodynamics account in which correlations between measurement shot/noise and radiation-pressure backaction generate the observed Raman sideband asymmetry.

Thus

`red/blue sideband asymmetry != mediator commutator`

without a complete measurement-model quotient.

## Frozen primary estimator

Use a weakly coupled two-level/narrow-band quantum spectrometer with transition frequency `Omega` and gravity-sensitive generalized force `F`.

Define the bath unsymmetrized spectrum

`S_FF(omega)=integral dt exp(i omega t)<F(t)F(0)>`.

In weak coupling,

`Gamma_down(Omega) proportional to S_FF(+Omega)`

`Gamma_up(Omega) proportional to S_FF(-Omega)`.

After separately measured detector/background rates are subtracted, freeze

`A_rate(Omega)=[Gamma_down-Gamma_up]/[Gamma_down+Gamma_up]`.

The inferred antisymmetric spectral component is

`rho_comm(Omega)=S(+Omega)-S(-Omega)`.

The transition-rate definition is primary because it directly ties opposite-frequency spectral weight to physical absorption/emission by a calibrated quantum probe rather than to a chosen photodetection ordering convention.

## Mandatory detector quotient

A KG commutator claim must include:

1. `dark/baseline rates`: source coupling disabled, detector operating point unchanged;
2. `readout-power extrapolation`: isolate components scaling with measurement backaction;
3. `classical injection`: inject stationary classical force/noise with matched `S_sym` and verify zero inferred intrinsic `rho_comm` after calibration;
4. `probe-state swap`: compare excitation from ground state and relaxation from excited state;
5. `frequency sweep`: infer the antisymmetric spectrum across several `Omega`, not one sideband pair;
6. `coupling geometry/sign reversal`: require source-mediated contribution to transform with the gravity-sensitive coupling;
7. `ordinary quantum bath ledger`: electromagnetic/phononic/optical quantum baths remain C4 and must be independently bounded;
8. `cross-detector check`: where possible compare local `A_rate` with the spatial ordered cross-spectrum `S^-_ij` proposed by T4-03.

## Classical stochastic benchmark

For a stationary real classical stochastic force with commuting values,

`C(t)=<F(t)F(0)>=C(-t)`

under the declared stationary autospectral assumptions, hence

`S_cl(+Omega)=S_cl(-Omega)`

and the **intrinsic mediator** contribution has

`A_rate=0`.

Detector/readout circuitry can still create a nonzero measured sideband ratio; the quotient above is designed precisely to remove that ambiguity.
