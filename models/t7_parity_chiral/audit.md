## Claim under attack

## Comparator result

Parity violation is not unique to a new KG parent.

Known gravitational EFT/modified-gravity constructions produce parity-odd sectors, including Chern-Simons-type couplings and parity-violating higher-derivative graviton interactions. These can generate helicity-dependent propagation, amplitude/phase birefringence and parity-odd graviton non-Gaussianity while remaining describable as an EFT extension with additional pseudoscalar/matter structure or higher-curvature interactions.

Therefore

`parity/chirality != automatically DeltaGamma_KG`.

The relevant comparator span includes

- C4 ordinary pseudoscalar/axion-like quantum fields coupled to curvature;
- modified-gravity scalar sectors;
- higher-derivative gravitational EFT operators;
- parity-asymmetric source/state preparation;
- detector/polarization systematics.

## Stronger future target

A chiral candidate would need a **gravity-attributed, state-controlled, Ward-complete relation** among

`{helicity asymmetry, source universality, ordered kernel, propagation birefringence, higher-point parity-odd vertex, low-energy EFT coefficients}`

that cannot be reproduced by the allowed parity-violating EFT/extra-field comparators.
