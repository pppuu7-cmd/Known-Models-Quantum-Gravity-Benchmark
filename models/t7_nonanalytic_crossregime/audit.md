# T7-05 Audit — nonanalytic / cross-regime residual class

Benchmark ID: `KMQGB-T7-M05-NONANALYTIC-CROSSREGIME`

State: TERMINAL

## Claim under attack

Potential shortcut:

> a nonanalytic momentum/frequency dependence such as logs, square roots, branch cuts or long-time power-law tails is automatically a beyond-C5 Candidate Gravity signature.

## Comparator result

This claim fails in the low-energy domain.

Quantum GR treated as an EFT has universal nonlocal/nonanalytic contributions generated by propagation of massless degrees of freedom, including gravitons. Standard examples include structures of the form

`q^2 log(-q^2)`, `sqrt(-q^2)` and related branch-cut/long-distance terms.

These terms are particularly important because they cannot be changed by arbitrary local counterterms, but that makes them **low-energy theorems of C5**, not automatically beyond-C5 residuals.

Likewise, thresholds and branch cuts can arise from ordinary additional particles/fields and multiparticle states in C4/C5/UV-completion comparators.

Therefore

`nonanalyticity != automatically DeltaGamma_KG`.

## Stronger cross-regime object

The scientifically useful target is the **origin-linked analytic structure**, not nonanalyticity alone.

For every proposed pole/cut/log/threshold freeze

`I_an={location,type,residue/discontinuity,spin/helicity content,source coupling,IR coefficient,UV/threshold origin,Ward/contact relation}`.

Then classify its origin:

1. massless C5 graviton/matter loop;
2. ordinary extra-field/multiparticle C4 threshold;
3. known EFT resummation/IR effect;
4. genuine new same-domain pole/cut/continuum not contained in the above.

Only case 4 can seed a future `DeltaGamma_KG`, and even then it must survive a broader UV/comparator family rather than merely differ from GR.

## Cross-regime guardrail

A relation linking low-energy coefficients to a UV pole/cut can be stronger than either sector separately, but it is accepted only when both theories/comparators are valid in the domain needed for the relation. Do not infer UV uniqueness by comparing a UV completion against C5 outside C5's validity regime.

## Terminal classification

`OPERATIONALLY_DEGENERATE`

The strongest surviving route is no longer a qualitative class such as non-Gaussianity, memory, chirality or nonanalyticity. Future model building must identify a **specific matched relation** across analytic structure, spin-2 Ward data and relational channel observables that remains outside the full applicable C5 and broader same-domain comparator span.
