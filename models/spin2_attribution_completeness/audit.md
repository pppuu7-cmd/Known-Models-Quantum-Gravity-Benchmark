# T6-01 Audit — massless spin-2 attribution completeness

Benchmark ID: `KMQGB-T6-M01-SPIN2-COMPLETENESS`

State: TERMINAL

## Frozen claim

Within the explicitly declared infrared/Minkowski scope

- one interacting long-range massless spin-2 field;
- locality / consistent factorization / no instantaneous signalling;
- unitarity;
- relativistic/luminal propagation and Lorentz/gauge consistency;
- universal leading coupling to conserved stress energy;
- leading low-derivative interaction sector;

the allowed leading graviton self-coupling and matter coupling collapse to the GR interaction structure. Higher-derivative EFT operators remain possible and are not excluded by this statement.

## Evidence boundary

Soft-graviton arguments force universal coupling. Locality-based analyses recover Lorentz structure and GR at the relevant order. BRST/deformation results show that, with at most two derivatives and the standard Pauli-Fierz free limit, consistent deformations are Einstein-Hilbert-type. Modern on-shell bootstrap work likewise finds the GR cubic graviton interaction plus higher-derivative corrections suppressed in the infrared.

The benchmark does **not** claim an assumption-free theorem that every conceivable massless-spin-2 theory is literally Einstein gravity. Relaxing locality, derivative order, background assumptions or other hypotheses can open additional constructions.

## Candidate Gravity consequence

A future KG model satisfying the frozen assumptions should treat the leading massless-spin-2/long-range sector as its **gravity-attribution anchor and C5 infrared baseline**, not as its novelty claim.

Novelty must instead survive in state/correlation/higher-CTP/UV structures while preserving the infrared spin-2 consistency relations.
