## Frozen observable claim under attack

Potential KG shortcut:

> a linked relation between symmetrized gravitational noise, commutator/retarded response and dissipation would prove a special quantum-gravity parent dynamics.

## Comparator result

For a stationary thermal/KMS quantum environment, the symmetric and antisymmetric/retarded two-point structures are not independent. In frequency space one obtains a fluctuation-dissipation relation of the schematic form

`N(omega) = coth(beta omega/2) Im D_R(omega)`

with tensor/projector factors and convention-dependent normalizations understood.

This relation is generic open-quantum-system/KMS structure. It is not unique to gravity and occurs for ordinary quantum fields and detector-field systems.

At zero temperature the corresponding vacuum spectral relation is recovered as the appropriate limit. In nonequilibrium, squeezed, explicitly time-dependent or nonstationary states, the equilibrium KMS formula must not be imposed blindly; more general nonequilibrium relations may replace it or no simple scalar FDR may exist.

## RQIR interpretation

A measured FDR/KMS relation can support

- quantum/environmental state characterization;
- consistency between noise and dissipation;
- an equilibrium/null test;

but it does not provide **gravity attribution**. A scalar, electromagnetic or other quantum bath can obey the same structural relation.

The gravity-specific layer must still come from the tensor/spin-2 source structure, universal stress-energy coupling and gravitational Ward/contact identities.

A future KG relation must combine the response/noise spectral relation with `G_attr`, or deliberately predict a controlled violation/deformation whose state and nonequilibrium origin cannot be absorbed by ordinary quantum environments.
